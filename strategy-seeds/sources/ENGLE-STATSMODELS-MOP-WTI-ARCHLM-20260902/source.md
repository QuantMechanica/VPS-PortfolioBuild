## Exact Mechanic

On the first executable `XTIUSD.DWX` D1 tick after a genuine broker-month
transition, reconstruct exactly sixty-one consecutive completed broker-month-
end closes `C[0]..C[60]`, oldest to newest. Exclude current-month prices and
form sixty chronological adjacent log returns:

```text
r[i] = ln(C[i+1]/C[i]), i=0..59
mean = sum(r[i])/60
e[i] = r[i]-mean
v[i] = e[i]^2 / (sum(e[j]^2)/60)

For t=6..59:
  y[t] = v[t]
  X[t] = [1, v[t-1], v[t-2], v[t-3], v[t-4], v[t-5], v[t-6]]

beta = ordinary least-squares minimizer of sum((y-X*beta)^2)
ybar = sum(y)/54
SST = sum((y-ybar)^2)
SSE = sum((y-X*beta)^2)
R2 = 1-SSE/SST
ARCH_LM = 54*R2
mom12 = sum(r[i], i=48..59)

BUY  iff ARCH_LM >= 4.73 and mom12 > +1e-12
SELL iff ARCH_LM >= 4.73 and mom12 < -1e-12
FLAT otherwise
```

Dividing squared residuals by their positive mean is a computation-preserving
conditioning step: the centered OLS R-squared is invariant when the dependent
and every nonconstant lag column share the same positive scale. Require
positive finite closes; finite returns and arithmetic; mean squared residual
above `1e-18`; a full-rank seven-column normal equation under scaled partial
pivoting; finite coefficients; `SST>1e-18`; and finite `R2` in `[0,1]` within
`1e-10` roundoff tolerance. Clamp only that stated tolerance.

The gate is directionless: it measures serial dependence in squared demeaned
monthly returns. Only the independently sourced newest twelve-month return
supplies trade direction. A nonqualifying gate, neutral direction, singular
regression, or invalid state consumes the month flat. Neither statistic nor
momentum magnitude changes risk.

The inclusive `4.73` boundary is the two-decimal pre-data approximation to
the median `4.734633677704946` from 200,000 independent sixty-observation
standard-normal paths generated by NumPy PCG64 seed `20260902`. The exact
card statistic was applied after demeaning. This is an empirical finite-sample
cadence divider, not an ARCH critical value, p-value, WTI calibration, or
profitability claim. The receipt is
`artifacts/qm5_wti_archlm_tr_null_density_20260902.json`.
