## Structural cause
The FTMO book holds no high-density, short-holding, low-swap FX sleeve.  Major
FX pairs are the deepest, tightest-spread instruments the farm trades; during the
London and New York session windows their intraday flow exhibits short-horizon
overextension-reversion: runs of consecutive same-direction M15 bars push price
beyond what session information flow sustains, and the close back through a short
EMA marks the inventory-clearing snap.  Confining entries to the two liquid
windows, bounding holding minutes, and forcing flat before each session close
removes the overnight gap and swap tails (swap ~ 0) while M15 density clears the
challenge's activity and progression requirements.
