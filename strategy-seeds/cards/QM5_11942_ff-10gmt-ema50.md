# FF — 10:00 GMT EMA 50 Strategy

Source: ForexFactory "10:00 GMT EMA50" London-continuation thread (lineage `forex_factory_unusual`).
Target symbols: EURUSD.DWX

## Thesis
By 10:00 GMT London has set its direction; the first M5 EMA-50 crossover after 10:00 GMT often begins a move that runs into the US open.

## Entry Rules
- **Timeframe:** M5, closed bar [1]. **Indicator:** `E = EMA(Close,50)`.
- **Arming:** the setup arms at 10:00 GMT each day; only the FIRST qualifying crossover of the day (after 10:00 GMT) is taken.
- **Buy:** first closed M5 bar after 10:00 GMT with `Close[1] > E[1]` AND `Close[2] <= E[2]` (cross up). Enter at that bar's close.
- **Sell:** first closed M5 bar after 10:00 GMT with `Close[1] < E[1]` AND `Close[2] >= E[2]` (cross down). Enter at that bar's close.
- One position per magic; one entry per day.

## Exit & Management
- **Stop Loss:** the opposite extreme of the crossover candle (`Low[1]` for Buys, `High[1]` for Sells). If that distance is < 5 pips, floor the stop at 20 pips.
- **Take Profit:** whichever comes first — **time exit at 14:00 GMT** OR a fixed **1.5 * initial risk** target.
- **Position Sizing:** RISK_FIXED (backtest) / RISK_PERCENT (live) tied to the stop distance.
