## 1. Strategy Logic

The EA evaluates the approved GARCH(1,1) one-step variance forecast on closed
D1 bars. A close outside the 1.5-sigma cone around `Open[1]`, confirmed by the
SMA(50), enters in the breakout direction. Initial risk is one forecast sigma,
the fixed target is 2R, and an open position's stop ratchets only tighter along
the closed-bar one-sigma cone (`Open[1] - sigma` for long positions and
`Open[1] + sigma` for short positions).

The bounded D1 state (one OHLC bar plus at most 100 closes) is refreshed before
new-bar admission. The card's `1.8 * ATR(14,D1)[1]` spread rule is then checked
again inside `Strategy_EntrySignal` immediately before an order is constructed.
`OnInit` rejects a chart outside the approved D1 timeframe, a symbol/slot pair
outside the three registered targets, or a mismatch with the declared V5
Friday-close safety override.

Entry/exit logic is encoded in the five `Strategy_*` hooks in
`QM5_37008_garch-volatility-forecast-breakout.mq5`. Framework wiring (risk, magic, news, Friday close)
is inherited from `QM_Common.mqh` and is not redocumented here.
