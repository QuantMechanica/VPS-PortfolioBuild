## Locked Rule

On the first processed XNG D1 bar after a genuine broker-month transition,
load exactly 253 completed closes and compute:

```text
r[d] = close[d] / close[d-1] - 1
mu = sum(r[d]) / 252
s2 = sum((r[d] - mu)^2) / 251
m4 = sum((r[d] - mu)^4) / 252
kurtosis = m4 / (s2^2)

BUY  when kurtosis > 3.0 + 1e-12
SELL when kurtosis < 3.0 - 1e-12
FLAT otherwise or when state is invalid
```

Require strictly increasing timestamps, positive finite closes, finite
returns and moments, `s2 > 1e-12`, and a newest completed endpoint before the
decision bar and no more than ten calendar days stale. Do not substitute log

Consume and persist the broker month before history, signal, spread, quote,
