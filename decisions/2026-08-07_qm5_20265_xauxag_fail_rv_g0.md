## Locked Rule

On each new `XAUUSD.DWX` D1 host bar, align exactly sixty-two completed
`XAUUSD.DWX` and `XAGUSD.DWX` timestamps and define:

```text
r[k]  = ln(XAU_close[k]) - ln(XAG_close[k])
r0    = ratio at completed shift 1 (newest)
r1    = ratio at completed shift 2 (prior)
upper = max(ratio at completed shifts 3..62)
lower = min(ratio at completed shifts 3..62)
```

The sixty channel observations must predate both event bars. Require positive
finite closes, exact timestamp alignment, finite ratios, `upper > lower`, and
no equality at a boundary.

- If `r1 > upper` and `lower < r0 < upper`, the upside break failed: SELL XAU
  and BUY XAG.
- If `r1 < lower` and `lower < r0 < upper`, the downside break failed: BUY XAU
  and SELL XAG.
- Otherwise remain flat.

Consume the newest D1 event bar before spread, quote, ATR, sizing, or order
gates so a rejected, failed, stopped, or restarted attempt cannot retry.
Maintain exactly zero or two opposite legs. One aggregate
`RISK_FIXED=1000` budget is split equally after each leg's independent frozen
`3.5*ATR(20,D1)` stop normalization. No take-profit, scale-in, or intrabar
entry is authorized.

For an open package, calculate the arithmetic mean of the newest twenty
synchronized completed log ratios. Close a short-ratio package when the newest
ratio is at or below that mean; close a long-ratio package when it is at or
above that mean. Also close on invalid synchronized state, invalid package or
stop composition, or after thirty calendar days. Friday close is disabled so
the multi-day relative-value path can complete; broker hard stops and the
framework kill switch remain binding.
