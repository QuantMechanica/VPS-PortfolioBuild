## Locked Rule

Align 64 completed XAU and XAG D1 bars by timestamp and define
`r_i = ln(XAU_i) - ln(XAG_i)`. For the current shifts 1-63 and prior shifts
2-64 independently, calculate the 63-value median, the median of absolute
deviations from that median, and:

```text
robust_z = 0.6744897501960817 * (latest_ratio - median) / MAD
```

Require positive finite closes, exact timestamp alignment, finite arithmetic,
and `MAD > 1e-12`. A current score crossing above `+2.0` from a prior score at
or below `+2.0` opens SELL XAU / BUY XAG. A crossing below `-2.0` from a prior
score at or above `-2.0` opens BUY XAU / SELL XAG. No entry is authorized
without a fresh crossing, and a consumed D1 attempt cannot retry after restart.

One aggregate `RISK_FIXED=1000` budget is split equally across legs after each
leg's own frozen `3.5*ATR(20,D1)` stop distance. Close on
`abs(current_robust_z) <= 0.5`, invalid state or package, or 45 elapsed calendar
days. Friday close is disabled so the relative-value package can complete a
multi-day convergence path; hard stops and the kill switch remain binding.
