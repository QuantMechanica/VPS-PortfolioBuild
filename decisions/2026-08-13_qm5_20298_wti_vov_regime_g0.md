## Locked Rule

On the first processed WTI D1 bar after a genuine broker-month transition,
load exactly 543 completed closes, newest first. For block offset `b`, where
`b=0` is recent and `b=271` is preceding, calculate 252 overlapping realized-
volatility observations:

```text
r[b,s,k] = ln(close[b+s+k] / close[b+s+k+1]), k = 0..19
rv[b,s]  = sample_std(r[b,s,0..19], denominator 19) * sqrt(252), s = 0..251
mean_rv[b] = average(rv[b,0..251])
vov[b] = sqrt(sum((rv[b,s] - mean_rv[b])^2) / 252) / mean_rv[b]

BUY  when vov[0] < vov[271] - 1e-12
SELL when vov[0] > vov[271] + 1e-12
FLAT otherwise or when state is invalid
```

The two blocks use disjoint log-return support: recent return indices
`0..270` and preceding return indices `271..541`, sharing only their boundary
close. Require strictly decreasing series indices to map to strictly older
timestamps, positive finite closes, finite returns and intermediate values,
positive inner sample variance, positive mean realized volatility, positive
VoV population variance, and a newest completed endpoint before the decision
bar and no more than ten calendar days stale.

Do not substitute implied data, raw volatility, variance ratio, a percentile,
overlapping block support, a fitted threshold, a trend or calendar filter,
score-sized risk, or an alternate direction. Consume and persist the broker
month before history, signal, spread, quote, news, ATR, sizing, or order gates.
Use one `RISK_FIXED=1000` WTI position with a frozen
`3.5 * ATR(20,D1)` hard stop, a 1,500-point entry-spread cap, and no take-
profit. Close before monthly replacement or after forty calendar days. Friday
close and both news axes are OFF. At most one registered WTI position exists.
