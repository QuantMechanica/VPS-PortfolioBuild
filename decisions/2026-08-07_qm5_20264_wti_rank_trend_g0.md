## Locked Rule

At the start of broker month `t`, take the thirteen consecutive completed
month-end closes `P_0..P_12` from months `t-13..t-1`, ordered oldest to newest.
Require every close to be positive and finite and require no exact tie between
any pair. For all `0 <= i < j <= 12`, define:

```text
pair(i,j) = +1 when P_j > P_i
            -1 when P_j < P_i
S         = sum(pair(i,j)) across all 78 pairs
tau       = S / 78
```

Require `abs(S) >= 28`. Buy WTI when `S > 0`; sell WTI when `S < 0`;
otherwise remain flat. With thirteen no-tie observations,
`Var(S) = 13*12*31/18 = 268.666...`; the continuity-corrected statistic at
`abs(S)=28` is `(28-1)/sqrt(Var(S)) = 1.647...`, fixing the conventional
two-sided ten-percent boundary before any pipeline result. Close before the
next monthly decision and do not retry within a consumed month.
