## Locked Rule

At the start of broker month `t`, take the thirteen consecutive completed
month-end closes from months `t-13` through `t-1`, ordered oldest to newest.
For `i = 0..12`, define:

```text
x_i   = i
y_i   = ln(close_i)
x_bar = 6
y_bar = average(y_i)
Sxx   = sum((x_i - x_bar)^2)
Sxy   = sum((x_i - x_bar) * (y_i - y_bar))
Syy   = sum((y_i - y_bar)^2)
beta  = Sxy / Sxx
R2    = (Sxy * Sxy) / (Sxx * Syy)
```

Require positive finite closes, consecutive month keys, finite arithmetic,
`Sxx > 0`, `Syy > 0`, `abs(beta) > 1e-10`, and `R2 >= 0.50`. Buy natural gas
when `beta > 0`; sell natural gas when `beta < 0`; otherwise remain flat.
Close before the next monthly decision and do not retry within a consumed
month.
