## Locked Rule

On the first processed WTI D1 bar after a genuine broker-month transition,
load exactly 505 completed closes, newest first. For block offset `b`, where
`b=0` is recent and `b=252` is preceding, calculate 252 simple returns, sort
the complete vector ascending, and average exactly the five largest values:

```text
r[b,k] = close[b+k] / close[b+k+1] - 1, k = 0..251
MAX[b] = arithmetic_mean(five_largest(r[b,0..251]))

BUY  when MAX[0] < MAX[252] - 1e-12
SELL when MAX[0] > MAX[252] + 1e-12
FLAT otherwise or when state is invalid
```

The blocks use disjoint return support: recent close-index pairs `0/1` through
`251/252`, and preceding pairs `252/253` through `503/504`. They share only
the boundary close at index 252. Require strictly decreasing series indices
to map to strictly older timestamps, positive finite closes, finite returns
and MAX values, exactly five selected order statistics per block, and a newest
completed endpoint before the decision bar and no more than ten calendar days
stale.

Do not substitute log returns, one maximum observation, a percentile,
winsorization, kurtosis, skewness, volatility-of-volatility, overlapping
blocks, a fitted threshold, a trend or calendar filter, score-sized risk, or
an alternate direction. Consume and persist the broker month before history,

1,500-point entry-spread cap, and no take-profit. Close before monthly
