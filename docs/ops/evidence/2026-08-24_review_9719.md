## Actionable defects (RECYCLE)

1. **Missing near-universal framework lifecycle/telemetry hooks.** The file ends
   at `OnTick`. It has no `OnTester` (present in 3791/3797 fleet EAs), no
   `QM_EquityStreamOnNewBar` call (3645/3797), no `OnTradeTransaction` forwarder
   (3644/3797), no `OnTimer` forwarder. `grep -c` for all four = 0 in the source.
   Missing equity stream risks empty Q04 stream lines -> INVALID; missing OnTester
   omits the canonical tester objective. Restore the standard V5 skeleton tail.

2. **OnTick ordering suspends exits and Friday-flatten during news blackout.** The
   news eligibility `return` (`if(!news_allows) return;`) precedes
   `QM_FrameworkHandleFridayClose()`, `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` (10-day time
   stop), and `Strategy_ExitSignal()` (median exit). During a news blackout an open
   position cannot be time-stopped, median-exited, or Friday-flattened. News must
   gate ENTRY only. Reorder: kill switch -> Friday close -> manage -> strategy exit
   -> entry-only news/spread eligibility -> new-bar gate -> entry.

3. **Symbol universe exceeds the approved card.** Card Target Symbols = exactly
   `SP500.DWX` (backtest), `NDX.DWX`, `WS30.DWX`, D1 index-only. The build
   generated 13 setfiles and activated 10 magic rows spanning FX majors
   (EUR/GBP/USDJPY/USDCHF/USDCAD/AUD/NZD), XAUUSD and extra indices. Card universe
   is a hard-bounded/ROT item; expansion needs an OWNER card amendment. Restrict to
   the three approved index targets or amend the card.

4. **No SPEC.md.** `framework/EAs/QM5_9719_.../SPEC.md` absent (present in 3051
   fleet EAs); `validate_spec_doc.py` fails. No durable operator contract.
