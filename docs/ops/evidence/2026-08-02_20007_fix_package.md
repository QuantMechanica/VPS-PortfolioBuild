`docs/ops/CODEX_BRIEF_2026-08-02_20007_fix_package.md` are implemented:

   matched line and log mtime.

   improve by at least one point, must satisfy the stricter broker stop/freeze
   distance, and is attempted at most once per bar. ORB and gold management
   remain per tick.

   read-only, without looking at `T_Live` or mutating terminal state. It reports
   the live T3 fault as `FAIL`.
