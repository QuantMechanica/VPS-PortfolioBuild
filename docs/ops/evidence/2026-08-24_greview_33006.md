## Findings

### 1. Build hardening fails the approved card contract

Read-only invocation:

```text
python tools/strategy_farm/build_gate_hardening.py --repo-root . --ea-label QM5_33006_andrea-unger-turtle-soup-false-breakout
```

Result: **FAIL**, four findings.

- The card requires a 2.0% daily realized-loss entry halt, 2.5% daily hard stop, and 5.0% total drawdown stop (`cards_approved/...md:86`, `:110-111`). The EA declares none of these and calls the legacy initializer at `framework/EAs/QM5_33006_andrea-unger-turtle-soup-false-breakout/QM5_33006_andrea-unger-turtle-soup-false-breakout.mq5:236-242`. The legacy framework supplies materially different generic values (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:296-298`). Hardening reports three `EA_CARD_LOSS_LIMIT_MISMATCH` failures.
- The card's rollover blackout is explicitly GMT (`cards_approved/...md:85`), but the EA compares raw broker `TimeCurrent()` at `.mq5:100-105`. No `QM_BrokerToUTC` conversion exists. Hardening reports `EA_BROKER_TIME_USED_FOR_GMT_WINDOW`.

Named fix: wire the card-declared 2.0%/2.5%/5.0% controls through the governed framework contract without changing those values, and convert broker time to UTC before applying 23:55–00:05 GMT.

### 2. Default risk mode is invalid and does not provide the required live contract

The source defaults both `RISK_PERCENT=0.5` and `RISK_FIXED=1000.0` at `.mq5:19-20`. `QM_FrameworkValidateRiskInputs` rejects the both-positive state at `framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:166-177`, so a default/live attach fails initialization. The three backtest setfiles correctly override `RISK_FIXED=1000` and `RISK_PERCENT=0` (`sets/*.set:17-20`), but there is no valid percent-live source default.

Named fix: retain the governed Q02 setfiles as fixed-risk and make the source/live default percent-risk with `RISK_FIXED=0`; validate the environment-to-risk-mode binding.

### 3. Mandatory no-trade windows do not govern already-live pending orders

The EA creates broker-side pending stops with 86,400-second expiration at `.mq5:179` and `:201`. `OnTick` returns on a news blackout at `.mq5:259-264`, on Friday close at `:266`, and on rollover/spread at `:267` before management at `:275`. Consequently an owned pending stop can fill during the mandatory news/GMT/Friday blackout. Friday handling closes positions but does not delete pending orders (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:938-956`).

Named fix: cancel owned pending orders fail-closed before every blackout/Friday return and use the governed `QM_TM_RemovePendingOrder` helper (`framework/include/QM/QM_TradeManagement.mqh:388-414`).

### 4. Trade management bypasses the V5 framework chain

Although `QM_Common.mqh` is included at `.mq5:6`, the EA additionally includes `Trade/Trade.mqh` at `:7` and directly calls `CTrade.OrderDelete` at `:71-72`. This bypasses the framework's centralized trade context, logging, and pending-removal path.

Named fix: remove the direct `CTrade` path and route pending removal through `QM_TM_RemovePendingOrder`.

### 5. Tick mechanics are not card-faithful

The card defines entry and stop offsets in ticks (`cards_approved/...md:90`, `:93`, `:97`). The EA substitutes `SYMBOL_POINT` at `.mq5:140` and computes `buffer=strategy_buffer_ticks*point` at `:160`; it then uses `2*buffer` for the fixed two-tick stop at `:169` and `:191`. Changing the entry-buffer input therefore also changes the stop distance, and point is not guaranteed to equal `SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE` on all three symbols.

Named fix: use `SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE` and keep the card's fixed two-tick stop independent from the configurable one-tick entry buffer.

### 6. Entry execution adds unapproved rules and the approved card is internally ambiguous

- The card's formula defines the 20-day extreme over `i=1..20` (`cards_approved/...md:74-76`) while the entry simultaneously requires bar `[1]` to breach that extreme (`:89-93`), which is impossible if `[1]` is included. The source silently changes the window to bars `[2..21]` via `start=2` at `.mq5:146-147`. This is economically plausible but not formally identical to the approved text.
- After the signal bar has closed back through the stop level, the EA accepts a long only when `buy_trigger > ask + point` and a short only when `sell_trigger < bid - point` (`.mq5:171`, `:193`). This undocumented next-quote gap condition drops otherwise valid card signals.
- The card specifies no 24-hour pending lifetime or dual-signal precedence. The source invents the expiry (`.mq5:179`, `:201`) and always returns the long first (`:162-181`) if an outside bar satisfies both sides.

Named fix: obtain an approved clarification for the prior-extreme window, already-crossed stop handling, dual-signal precedence, and pending lifetime; then implement exactly that frozen contract.
