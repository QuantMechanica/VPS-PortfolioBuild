1. **Anti-cluster state is not restart-persistent.** The card explicitly requires persistence across restarts (`card:110`). The EA stores only process-local globals (`mq5:59-60`), updates them during signal construction before order success (`:137`, `:162`), and has no `OnInit` reconstruction or durable state. **Named fix:** reconstruct the last successful same-direction entry from durable owned-deal/position history (or a governed persistent store) on initialization, update only after confirmed entry, and fail closed when reconstruction is unavailable.

2. **The gap-bar-open news condition is absent.** The card requires skipping a signal when the gap bar's open is inside a high-impact news window (`card:70-75`). The only executable entry news check tests `broker_now=TimeCurrent()` at the next bar (`mq5:276`, `:301-307`); `Strategy_NewsFilterHook` is inert (`:230-233`). It never tests `bar1.time`, the gap bar's opening timestamp. **Named fix:** query the governed news calendar for the captured gap-bar-open broker time in addition to retaining the ordinary current entry blackout/compliance gate.

3. **Raw series calls lack the required performance annotations.** Direct `iBarShift`/`iClose` calls at `mq5:120`, `:145`, `:194`, `:200`, and `:203` carry no `// perf-allowed:` justification. **Named fix:** use framework readers where available and add the required bounded/O(1) `perf-allowed` annotations to every justified raw-series exception.

The gap-origin target is recomputed from the position-open bar (`mq5:200-203`) rather than copied into mutable strategy state. Its shift advances with elapsed D1 bars and therefore continues to address the same historical origin close; this is algebraically stable in ordinary history and is not a separate blocker.
