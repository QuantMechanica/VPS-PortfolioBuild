## Card-fidelity findings

The EA is salvageable, but it is not formally clean for Q02. The following are actionable review defects.

1. **Triangle geometry is not the approved slope mechanic.** The card requires `|beta_res| <= 0.05` and `beta_supp >= +0.10` (`card:74-77`). The EA captures pivot bar indices but never uses them (`mq5:132-160`); it instead compares the two pivot prices as a relative percentage and merely checks `l1 > l2` / `h1 < h2` (`mq5:170-188`). There is no price-over-bar slope or minimum `0.10` converging-boundary slope. The EA and all setfiles also default `strategy_pivot_window=4` (`mq5:37`; each setfile line 25), while the approved card declares `InpPivotWindow=5` (`card:160-165`). **Required fix:** implement time-normalized boundary slopes, enforce the approved minimum trend slope symmetrically, and restore the approved pivot-window default.

2. **Exit management substitutes different mechanics.** The card names a take-profit of one triangle height and trailing at swing pivots (`card:96-99`). The EA never calculates triangle height; it sets TP to `strategy_tp_rr * SL distance` (`mq5:245-267`). Its management hook performs a one-time break-even move only (`mq5:277-325`) and never trails the latest swing pivot. **Required fix:** obtain card adjudication for the card's internally contradictory “one height (1:2)” wording, then implement the authorized triangle-height/2R admission rule and actual tightening-only swing-pivot trail.

3. **Mandatory volume confirmation can fail open and changes the comparator.** The card requires strict `Vol[1] > 1.3 * SMA(Vol,20)[1]` (`card:90-94`). The EA accepts `avg_vol <= 0.0` and uses `>=` (`mq5:114-130`). **Required fix:** reject missing/zero mandatory volume and use the card's strict comparison.

4. **Card-declared capital controls are absent.** The card declares a 2.0% realized-daily entry halt, 2.5% daily hard stop, 5.0% total drawdown stop, 0.50% live per-trade risk, and maximum three-tick slippage (`card:83-88`, `card:103-114`, `card:160-165`). None of the three loss limits or the deviation ceiling is implemented in the EA. The canonical `build_gate_hardening.py` invocation reports three `EA_CARD_LOSS_LIMIT_MISMATCH` failures. **Required fix:** wire the exact loss rails through the governed framework interfaces, cap percent-mode live risk at 0.50%, and configure the three-tick deviation ceiling without changing backtest fixed-risk economics.

5. **The GMT blackout uses raw broker time.** The card specifies 23:55-00:05 GMT (`card:83-87`), but `StrategyInRolloverWindow(TimeCurrent())` compares the unconverted broker clock (`mq5:60-72`, `mq5:202-205`). The hardening gate reports `EA_BROKER_TIME_USED_FOR_GMT_WINDOW`. **Required fix:** convert broker time through the governed UTC/DST-aware framework reader before comparing the window.

6. **Entry filters use stale/unseeded state and suppress active management.** `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` executes before management and before the new-bar state refresh (`mq5:372-415`). On startup, `g_last_atr=0` causes the spread ceiling to be skipped (`mq5:54`, `mq5:212-217`); on a new bar it checks the previous cached ATR and is not re-run after refresh. Rollover or wide spread returns before `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` (`mq5:385-388`). **Required fix:** seed restart-safe state, refresh the completed-bar ATR before entry admission, and manage existing risk before entry-only filters.

7. **Invalid pivot-stop geometry silently changes the stop model.** If the card's pivot stop lies on the wrong side of entry, the EA substitutes `1.5 * ATR` (`mq5:245-266`). **Required fix:** reject the entry when pivot-stop geometry is invalid; do not replace the governed pivot stop with ATR.
