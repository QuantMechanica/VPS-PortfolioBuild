## Blocking findings

### 1. No approved Strategy Card of record

`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_12921*.md` has no match. The
only card is physically governed under `cards_rejected`. Its stale internal
`g0_status: APPROVED` field at line 13 does not restore approval authority.
`build_gate_hardening.py` independently resolved `card_path: null` and emitted
the missing-card warnings. Therefore there is no approved contract against
which this EA can be declared formally clean for Q02.

Named fix: obtain an OWNER-authorized approved Strategy Card first. Re-review
the implementation against that approved byte artifact; do not promote the
rejected file merely from its stale frontmatter.

### 2. January return is sampled from the wrong completed bars

The rejected card says to calculate SP500 January total return from the final
December close to the final January close
(`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_rejected/QM5_12921_qp-january-barometer-card.md:39`).
The EA runs its calendar update only after a new D1 bar is detected
(`framework/EAs/QM5_12921_qp-january-barometer-card/QM5_12921_qp-january-barometer-card.mq5:212`),
then calls the update at source line 216, but:

- line 67 captures `PRICE_OPEN` with shift 1 on the first January bar. That is
  the prior completed December bar's **open**, not the required final December
  close (nor the current first-January open).
- line 69 refreshes the January close with shift 1 only while
  `current_month == 1`. On the first February bar the code takes the
  `current_month == 2` branch without one final refresh, so the sampled value
  is the penultimate January session's close rather than the final January
  close.

Both endpoints can change the sign of the annual signal.

Named fix: after card approval, derive both endpoints from explicitly bounded,
complete D1 history: the final December close and final January close. Fail
closed if the January sequence is incomplete, and add deterministic boundary
tests for year/month transitions and missing sessions.

### 3. The sole annual entry opportunity is consumed before successful entry

`g_strategy_entry_due` is reset at source line 110 before timeframe/parameter,
position, quote, ATR-stop, and order-placement validation. The actual
`QM_TM_OpenPosition` call is not until line 245, and its result is not used to
restore or durably record the pending annual signal. A transient quote, stop,
or trade failure therefore burns the only February entry opportunity for the
whole year. The in-memory calendar state at lines 40-46 is also not rebuilt on
restart, so a restart after January can miss the annual transition entirely.

Named fix: make the calendar decision restart-reconstructable and idempotent;
consume the annual pending state only after confirmed owned-position creation
(or preserve a bounded retry state), with tests for restart and failed order
placement.

### 4. Default Friday close contradicts the February-to-December hold

The card requires a year-end exit
(`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_rejected/QM5_12921_qp-january-barometer-card.md:45`),
while the EA
defaults `qm_friday_close_enabled = true` at source line 30 and invokes
`QM_FrameworkHandleFridayClose()` at line 204. The generated backtest setfiles
do not override that input. Because the entry flag is one-shot, the first
Friday close collapses an intended roughly eleven-month position to at most a
week and the EA does not re-enter.

Named fix: the approved card and governed setfiles must explicitly authorize
the weekend-hold policy. For the currently described year-long mechanic,
disable Friday close and verify the framework exit remains the year-end/stop
path.

### 5. The delivered baseline universe exceeds the card's authority

The only located card is titled for SP500 and defines the baseline on
SP500.DWX
(`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_rejected/QM5_12921_qp-january-barometer-card.md:28`).
Its lines 40-41 define the SP500 baseline, and line 74 permits NDX/WS30 only
as a later T6 parallel-validation caveat after SP500 passes the earlier gates.
The build nevertheless registers and generates Q02 backtest sets for SP500,
NDX, WS30, GDAXI, and UK100. Current registry rows are
`framework/registry/magic_numbers.csv:17610-17614`; the extra setfile symbols
are visible at each setfile's line 7. GDAXI and UK100 have no support in the
located card, and NDX/WS30 are premature baseline expansions.

Named fix: generate only the symbol universe explicitly authorized by the new
approved card. Any broader multi-index thesis requires upstream card approval,
not a SPEC-only expansion.
