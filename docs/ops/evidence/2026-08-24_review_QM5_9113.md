## Defects (actionable — RECYCLE)

1. **[High] Entry-side filters suppress the strategy exit.** `OnTick()` returns on the
   news decision (mq5:271-274) and on `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` (mq5:277, which bundles
   timeframe + history + spread *entry* eligibility) BEFORE `Strategy_ExitSignal()`
   (mq5:284). The card requires the opposite-zero-cross close to fire on each completed
   D1 bar; during a news blackout, wide spread, or missing history an open position is
   left unmanaged by the strategy exit. Fix: keep Friday-close, management, and strategy
   exit reachable independent of every new-entry filter; gate news/spread only immediately
   before building a new entry request. (Broker-side ATR SL still caps catastrophic risk,
   so this is behavioral deviation, not uncapped risk.)

2. **[Medium] Rolling-window re-seed replaces the sealed recursive state.** Card seeds
   the alpha-beta state from the first available close and advances once per completed D1
   close. `Strategy_CalculateABVelocity()` (mq5:127-158) re-reads only the last
   `warmup+10` bars and re-seeds pos/vel from the oldest bar of that moving window on
   every call, so the initial condition shifts as the window advances — window-dependent
   zero-cross timing rather than one canonical recursive state.

3. **[Medium] SPEC.md is control-byte corrupted.** 7 non-whitespace control bytes
   confirmed at offsets 75,102,428,448,1915,2949,3166 (0x07/0x1b/0x08/0x0c); they eat
   leading chars of the equations, slug, source id, and paths (e.g. "lpha", "eta",
   "ramework"). `validate_spec_doc.py` still reports PASS. Regenerate SPEC from literal
   text with zero control bytes.
