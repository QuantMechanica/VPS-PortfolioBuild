### 2. DSS mechanism does not implement the approved-card formula

The approved card defines `DSS = EMA(EMA(Stoch_K(13,1),8),8)` (`card:39`) and then `Trigger = SMA(DSS,3)` (`card:40`). The source instead:

- aliases `p3` to `strategy_dss_stoch_period` (`mq5:117-120`),
- builds `k1` as an inner EMA of raw stochastic values (`mq5:130-135`),
- performs a second min/max stochastic normalization of `k1` over `p3` (`mq5:138-150`), and only then
- applies the outer EMA to that re-normalized series (`mq5:153`).

That is materially different from the declared double EMA of one stochastic series. Fix `DSS_ComputeAtShift` to implement the card formula exactly; do not add a second stochastic transform unless the Strategy Card is separately re-approved.

### 3. Exit/management state is neither transactional nor restart-safe

- Directional cooldown is consumed inside `Strategy_EntrySignal` (`mq5:281,293`) before `QM_TM_OpenPosition` is attempted (`mq5:511-515`). A rejected order therefore suppresses later valid entries for `strategy_cooldown_bars`. Move the cooldown commit to confirmed successful entry creation.
- `QM_TM_PartialClose` returns `bool` (`framework/include/QM/QM_TradeManagement.mqh:422-425`), but its result is ignored (`mq5:354-359,385-390`). `g_trade_state.t1_hit` becomes true even if half-volume is invalid or the close fails, enabling the post-T1 trail without the card-required 50% close. Mark T1 complete only after a confirmed partial close; otherwise retain retry/fail-closed state.
- `OnInit` zeroes `g_trade_state` (`mq5:439-445`). When an existing position is first seen, the code forces `t1_hit=false` and derives its T1 price from transient `g_long_t1/g_short_t1` (`mq5:312-320`), which are zero after restart unless a fresh same-direction signal occurs (`mq5:235-248`). Reconstruct T1 deterministically from durable entry data/state, or fail closed without silently disabling the card exit.
- `Strategy_NoTradeFilter` returns early on unready state or wide spread (`mq5:256-262`) and is called before `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition` (`mq5:474-476`). Entry-only spread/warmup filters must not block time-stop, reverse-crossover, partial-close, or trailing management. Run owned-position management before entry filters.

### 4. Indicator-buffer proof does not meet the review contract

The current source uses fixed buffers `k1[128]`, `rawk[64]`, and `rawk2[64]` (`mq5:122-124`) and hard-coded caps/guard `MathMin(...,128)` and `s < 128` (`mq5:126-142`).
