## Result

The Q04 low-frequency PF rule is now two-sided. A PF measurement cannot satisfy the positive floor when either:

- it is the exact `999.0` zero-gross-loss sentinel or is non-finite; or
- it is at least `25.0` on fewer than `15` trades per represented OOS year.

The ceiling is conditional on sample size, not a global PF cap. The diagnosis corpus contained 4,468 Q04 rows since 2026-07-01. Fifty-nine rows had a fold PF at least 25; every such fold in the current evidence population had at most 11 trades. The existing low-frequency boundary is fewer than 15 trades/year, so `PF >= 25 AND trades < 15 * OOS-years` isolates the denominator-dominated region without rejecting well-sampled results.

`999.0` is generated only when the profit-factor denominator has no losses. Q04's other no-measurement states are `None`/missing and non-finite PF; `0.0` remains an ordinary below-floor result. No second finite sentinel is emitted by the Q04 runner.

The strict-fold aggregator returns `FAIL` before `PASS_SOFT` arithmetic when a fold PF is unusable. The pooled low-frequency aggregator applies the same guard against the three-year sample-size boundary. The existing `lowfreq_pooled_pf_below_floor` result and reason are unchanged.
