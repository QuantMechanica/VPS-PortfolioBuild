## Blocking findings

### 1. Card-required one-shot/re-arm state is absent; `strategy_rearm_pct` is mechanically dead

The approved card requires the compression episode to be consumed after a breakout and re-armed only after width exceeds the rolling re-arm percentile (`card:61`), explicitly requiring a maintained `in_compression_episode` state (`card:114`) and making re-arm percentile a P3 parameter (`card:79`).

The EA declares `strategy_rearm_pct` at source line 42, but its only reads are the parameter-validity comparisons at lines 79-80. Entry recomputes only the prior-bar compression predicate at lines 199-218 and contains no episode state, consumption, or re-arm-percentile calculation. Consequently changing the declared input does not change strategy mechanics and the same volatility episode can become eligible again without the card-required `> rearm percentile` release. This violates the hard dead-input rule and the card's reviewer checkpoint (`card:116`).

**Required repair:** implement restart-safe one-shot episode consumption and re-arm logic driven by `strategy_rearm_pct`; add focused tests proving (a) a consumed episode cannot re-enter, (b) sub-threshold width does not re-arm, and (c) width above the configured percentile does re-arm.

### 2. The time stop counts calendar seconds, not 30 trading days

The approved contract is “30 trading days from entry” (`card:65`). Source lines 300-320 implement `strategy_max_hold_bars * PeriodSeconds(D1)` against `TimeCurrent() - POSITION_TIME`, so weekends and market closures count toward the stop. A Friday entry, for example, consumes two nominal D1 periods before another trading bar exists.

**Required repair:** derive elapsed closed D1 trading bars from bar time/index (with a defined entry-bar convention), not wall-clock seconds; test a weekend/holiday boundary.

### 3. Raw-series performance and management-reachability contracts are not met

Source uses raw `iClose` loops at lines 98 and 122, plus direct raw OHLC reads at lines 225-227 and 306-308, with no `perf-allowed` annotation. The exit path calls `Strategy_GetBBLevels(1)` and the raw OHLC reads on every tick because `Strategy_ExitSignal()` runs at source lines 397-409 before the new-bar gate at lines 419-420. This is materially more than a fixed once-per-bar exception: it recomputes a 20-close standard deviation on every tick while a position is open.

In addition, `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` returns before management/exit at source lines 392-397. Its spread branch at lines 171-181 can therefore suppress the card's midband/time-stop exits whenever the declared nonzero spread limit is exceeded. Entry filters must not make owned-position management unreachable.

**Required repair:** cache/copy bounded closed-bar data behind one D1 new-bar gate (with explicit copy-count/`ArraySize` guards), or add a narrowly justified `perf-allowed` annotation only after making the execution cadence bounded. Keep position management reachable independently of entry/news filters.

### 4. Implemented symbol universe does not match the approved card

The card's target list includes `XTIUSD` and does not list `GDAXI.DWX` or `UK100.DWX` (`card:81-82`). `Strategy_SymbolAllowed` includes GDAXI/UK100 and omits XTIUSD at source lines 55-69. The governed registry/setfile cohort likewise has 13 rows/files for GDAXI/UK100 but none for XTIUSD (`framework/registry/magic_numbers.csv:17202-17214`).

**Required repair:** align source, magic registry, resolver, and governed setfiles with the approved target universe, or obtain an approved card change before expanding/reducing it. Registry/resolver changes must use their governed generators and remain collision-free.
