## Blocking findings

### 1. HA reconstruction contradicts the approved card

The card defines the recursive HA open from its previous HA open/close (`card:48-53`) and makes
the restart/build requirement explicit: re-derive from history at attach and read **at least 200
bars plus one seed bar (201 bars)** (`card:104-109`).

The EA instead invents `strategy_ha_seed_bars=50` (`mq5:58`), starts each calculation at
`2 + strategy_ha_seed_bars`, and re-seeds there from that raw bar (`mq5:67-87`). With the delivered
default this reads shifts 52 through 1 and moves the seed origin forward on every D1 bar. The input
is not one of the card's declared P3 candidates (`card:75`) but is exported into every governed
setfile (representative GDAXI set: `:28-34`), making a non-card mechanic perturbable in later gates.

Named fix: remove the unapproved rolling seed input (or obtain an approved card amendment),
reconstruct the recursive HA state from at least the card-required 201 closed D1 bars with a
stable historical seed, update governed setfiles/SPEC, and recompile via governed `COMPILE_EA`.

### 2. Anti-cluster state does not survive `OnInit`

The card requires a per-direction last-entry timestamp and explicitly says that state **must
survive `OnInit`** (`card:57`, `card:108`). The EA stores only two process-local globals initialized
to zero (`mq5:60-61`), writes them on signals (`mq5:165-212`), and performs no reconstruction in
`OnInit` (`mq5:281-302`). An attach/restart inside the three-bar window therefore disables the
approved anti-cluster constraint until the next in-process entry.

Named fix: after framework magic initialization, reconstruct the most recent owned long/short
entry bar from durable deal/history evidence (fail closed if the required history cannot be
established), then add a restart-focused unit/static test and recompile via governed `COMPILE_EA`.

### 3. Governed symbol surface omits the card's primary oil target

The card repeatedly names oil CFD among the intended/primary symbols (`card:24`, `card:28`,
`card:86`). The delivered SPEC instead lists 13 symbols without oil and rejects every symbol not
on that list (`SPEC.md:37-56`). Registry rows `framework/registry/magic_numbers.csv:17280-17292`
and the 13 delivered setfiles match that narrower list; there is no `XTIUSD.DWX` row or setfile,
although `dwx_symbol_matrix.csv` recognizes `XTIUSD.DWX` (confirmed by the hardening check).

Named fix: allocate the XTIUSD slot through the governed registry path, regenerate
`QM_MagicResolver.mqh`, generate the governed XTIUSD D1 backtest setfile, correct SPEC, validate the
registry/setfile surface, and recompile via governed `COMPILE_EA`. If oil was intentionally removed,
the approved card must be amended by its owner rather than silently narrowed by the build.

### 4. SPEC parameter table is materially stale

`SPEC.md:24-33` says there are no strategy-specific inputs, while source declares seven at
`mq5:51-58` and every setfile binds them. `validate_spec_doc.py` checks structure and therefore
passes, but this content is not an accurate implementation spec. Correct it as part of recycle.
