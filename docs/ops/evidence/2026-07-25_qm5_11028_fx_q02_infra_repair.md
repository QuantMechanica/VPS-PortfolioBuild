## Failure analysis

EA review `3f34d832-4744-4827-9da3-ccccf301061e` rejected only the smoke
outcome. It recorded a zero-byte GBPJPY report and a T8 tester journal
contaminated by a concurrent UK100 job, explicitly classifying the result as
infrastructure contention rather than an MQ5 defect.

The later Q02 wave exhausted retries across four FX symbols. Its retained
database evidence includes `REPORT_MISSING`, `METATESTER_HUNG`, and
`INCOMPLETE_RUNS`; report directories were subsequently removed by retention.
The June binary also predates the current framework.

Source inspection found a deterministic throughput defect that amplified the
tester problem: while a position was open, `Strategy_ExitSignal()` read four
WMA buffers plus the completed close on every Model-4 tick even though the
card defines the signal on completed H1 bars. The old wiring also placed the
central news return above position management and strategy exits.

## Repair

Strategy mechanics, parameters, symbols, registry rows, and risk sizing are
unchanged.

- `QM_FrameworkTrackOpenPositionMae()` now runs before every early return.
- The framework new-bar event is consumed once and reused for the
  completed-bar opposite-signal exit and entry paths.
- WMA exit reads therefore execute once per completed H1 bar, not per tick.
- Position management and opposite-signal exits remain active through central
  news blackouts; the news gate suppresses new exposure only.
- Entry requests are zero-initialized before the strategy hook.
- All four setfiles were regenerated with the repository generator. Each
  declares the active magic slot, `RISK_FIXED=1000`, `RISK_PERCENT=0`, and all
  approved strategy inputs explicitly.
- The approved card is copied into the EA package and the SPEC revision
  history records this recovery.
