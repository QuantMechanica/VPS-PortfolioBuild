### 2. Gate 1 does not implement the declared Williams-fractal median pivot bands

The card declares Williams 3-bar fractal pivot detection (`card:60`) and median pivot bands `U`/`L` over a 60-to-300 H4-bar window (`card:62-68`). The EA instead uses the absolute highest high and lowest low from raw bars (`.mq5:210-246`); there is no fractal/pivot detector or median calculation anywhere in the source. Its duration scan is also capped at 120 bars (`.mq5:212`) even though the approved window extends to 300.

This changes the strategy’s defining range mechanism and therefore is not card-faithful.

**Named fix:** implement bounded Williams 3-bar pivot extraction, derive `U`/`L` from the declared median pivot bands across the full authorized window, and keep explicit read-count/array-size guards.

### 3. Gate 6 is logically too permissive and omits the pullback-pivot alternative

The card requires the current high to be within 0.5 ATR of either broken support `L` or the most recent significant pivot high in the pullback (`card:93-97`). The condition at `.mq5:285-288` rejects only highs materially *below* `L`; any high above `L + 0.5 ATR` passes because the second conjunct becomes false. No pullback pivot high is calculated.

**Named fix:** require absolute distance `<= tolerance` from `L` OR from a mechanically defined most-recent pullback pivot high.

### 4. The two-tier exit and 60% partial close are absent

The card requires TP1 at one range amplitude, a 60% partial close at TP1, break-even on the remaining 40%, and TP2 at 1.5 range amplitudes (`card:128-133`). The EA assigns the whole position a server TP at TP1 (`.mq5:337-348`). Its management hook only attempts a break-even move after profit reaches TP1 (`.mq5:360-390`); it contains no partial close and no TP2. In normal execution, the full-position TP closes the trade before a runner can exist.

**Named fix:** implement restart-safe 60% partial realization at TP1, move the surviving 40% to break-even, and target that runner at TP2.

### 5. Range-reuse and exit state is volatile across restart

The card’s range-validity rule permits only one entry per Phase-D break (`card:155-158`). The source stores the last range identity and `L/U` only in process globals (`.mq5:58-61`, assigned at `.mq5:350-353`), and `OnInit` does not reconstruct them (`.mq5:433-440`). A terminal/EA restart therefore permits the same range to be reused and also disables the range-dependent early failure/TP management for an already-open position.

**Named fix:** persist or deterministically reconstruct the active/consumed range identity and open-position `L/U` state on initialization, with a fail-closed fallback.
