  (`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_35008_short-term-bollinger-reversion-system.md:82-87`).

### R4 — mandatory 23:00 UTC exit can deassert while exposure remains

- The card requires a 23:00 GMT time exit
  (`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_35008_short-term-bollinger-reversion-system.md:95-98`).
- `Strategy_ExitSignal()` is true only for `23:00 <= hhmm < 23:55`
  (`framework/EAs/QM5_35008_short-term-bollinger-reversion-system/QM5_35008_short-term-bollinger-reversion-system.mq5:210-217`).
  A quote gap or rejected close can leave a position open when the condition
  becomes false at 23:55 and remains false after midnight.

Named fix: latch the due time-exit by position/day and continue governed close
retries until the owned position is confirmed absent, including across
rollover/restart.

mismatch.
