## Blocking findings

### 1. Card-required TP1, partial close, break-even, and KAMA runner are absent

The approved card requires a 50% TP1 at `+1.0 * ATR`, moving the remaining stop
to break-even, then trailing the runner with KAMA until the opposite cross
(`strategy-seeds/cards/approved/QM5_36008_nnfx-gold-kama-vortex-supertrend.md:95-98`).
The EA initializes `req.tp=0.0` and never assigns a TP
(`framework/EAs/QM5_36008_nnfx-gold-kama-vortex-supertrend/QM5_36008_nnfx-gold-kama-vortex-supertrend.mq5:237-293`),
while `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` is an empty stub at lines 296-298. The only
implemented discretionary exit closes the whole position at lines 300-347. It
also invents a Vortex reversal exit at lines 333-343 even though the card names
the KAMA runner exit. This materially changes both payoff shape and holding time.

**Required fix:** implement the card's restart-safe 50% TP1 / break-even state and
KAMA runner management using governed QM trade-management helpers; remove the
unapproved Vortex-only runner exit unless the approved card is formally amended.

### 2. Card loss limits are missing; the generic defaults contradict the card

The card requires a 2.0% realized-daily-loss entry halt, 2.5% daily hard stop,
and 5.0% total drawdown stop (card lines 82-87 and 110-113). The EA's no-trade
filter checks only rollover and spread at source lines 218-235 and never
initializes those card thresholds. The legacy framework initializer instead
calls `QM_KillSwitchInit(..., 3.0, 0.0, 1.0)`
(`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:296-298`), so total-DD protection is disabled
and the daily threshold is 3.0%, not the approved values.

Independent `build_gate_hardening.py` output confirms three
`EA_CARD_LOSS_LIMIT_MISMATCH` failures: missing `daily_entry_halt=2%`,
`daily_hard_stop=2.5%`, and `total_drawdown_stop=5%` inputs/wiring.

**Required fix:** wire the three approved card controls without changing their
thresholds, and make the 2.0% control entry-only while the 2.5%/5.0% hard stops
remain able to protect open positions.

### 3. Entry filters suspend management and protective exits

`OnTick()` returns on `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` at source lines 401-402 before
calling management/exit at lines 404-418. During the 23:55-00:05 GMT rollover
blackout or a spread spike, an existing trade cannot reach its management or
card exit logic. These are entry filters, not authority to suspend protection.

**Required fix:** evaluate open-position management and protective exits before
entry-only rollover/spread/news gating; keep the gates in front of new entries.

### 4. Card slippage tolerance is not wired

The approved card caps market-order slippage at 3 ticks (card line 113). The EA
does not expose or pass a card-specific deviation. `QM_FrameworkInit` configures
entry deviation to the generic 20 points (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:296`;
`framework/include/QM/QM_Entry.mqh:63-68,88,452-462`). No source evidence binds
that generic point value to the card's 3-tick limit.

**Required fix:** bind the card's 3-tick market-order tolerance through the
governed entry API with correct symbol tick-size conversion.
