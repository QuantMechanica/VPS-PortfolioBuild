  framework-layer interaction effects (shared indicator-buffer collisions, magic conflicts)

  context_evidence. Given the return claim is refuted ~2× and the frequency claim ~4–8×,
  commissioning a build purely for interaction-effect confirmation is low-value and is

  substantive refutation captured for that decision.
