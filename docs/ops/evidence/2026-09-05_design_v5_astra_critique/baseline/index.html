The mechanics we model

The behaviour behind the families.

Three repeatable structures sit under most of the archive. The charts are illustrative synthetic price action, calibrated to each instrument's real statistics — never a live quote.

EUR/USD · ICT / SMT session model

Trading the handoff between sessions.

This family reads the daily handoff between the Asian, London and New York sessions. It waits for liquidity to be swept beyond a prior session's high or low, then for a displacement move that confirms the turn. Entries sit against the swept liquidity, and the session that built the level defines the risk.

Illustrative price action (synthetic, calibrated) — not live.

US 100 · Indicator regime

Following the trend while the regime holds.

Here a stack of exponential moving averages sets the direction, and a Bollinger band measures whether the market is trending or ranging. The model only takes trades in the direction the moving-average stack agrees on, and stands aside when the band contracts into an undecided regime. It is a trend-follower with an explicit off-switch.

Illustrative price action (synthetic, calibrated) — not live.

XAU/USD · Mean reversion & range breakout

Fading the stretch, riding the squeeze.

Gold spends long stretches inside a range and then leaves it violently. Two complementary ideas cover both states: a Monday-range fade that sells the week's first over-extension back toward the mean, and a daily-squeeze breakout that buys the release once volatility has compressed. Each is a statistical bet on a well-documented tendency, not a prediction of one move.

Illustrative price action (synthetic, calibrated) — not live.
