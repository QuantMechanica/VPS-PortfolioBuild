What we do not publish

The archive is deliberately open about what a strategy does and how it was tested, and deliberately closed about the parts that would let it be copied. For every record we withhold:

  • Source code and the trading logic in executable form.
  • Parameter names, parameter values and set-file settings.
  • Per-strategy performance statistics and equity curves.
  • Internal identifiers, position sizing and account details.

What you see — the mechanism in words, the markets tested and the gate outcomes — is the honest evidence trail without the recipe.
