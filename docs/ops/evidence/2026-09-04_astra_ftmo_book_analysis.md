Re-score after true FTMO costs and blackouts, then measure the incremental joint-path benefit at the same loss budget. Refute an addition if it fails V4 certification or does not improve held-out pass probability/time without worsening the loss-bound criterion. Family-count growth alone is not that evidence.

**Do not infer a free speed gain from position size.** The historical overlap-floor experiment moves stage-1 pass **30% → 60%** at **0.50× → 1×**, but breach **4% → 30%**. It fails the requested “without raising failure probability” premise. Reduced stage-2 risk can protect against losses but also miss the internal clock; it must be tested, not presumed beneficial. [Sequential sweep](2026-09-04_astra_ftmo_legacy_first_passage.json).

**Reuse DXZ operational learning and observe both stages sequentially.** Retain the frozen-input self-test, exact binary/set attribution, common exposure coordination and lessons from the prior stopped campaign. Do not import its sizing, old standalone Sharpe or virtual payout EV as FTMO acceptance. Phase transition, flat confirmation, stage-2 provisioning and first-reward checks can be prepared in advance; their actual administrative waiting time is unmeasured and excluded from the trading-time table. The existing next-day reset convention is already a simplifying assumption.
