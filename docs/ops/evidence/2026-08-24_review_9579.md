   `QM_FrameworkDeclareExecutionContract(PERIOD_D1, …)`. That helper
   (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:474`) is the Card-v2 contract that fails closed on a
   wrong-TF chart attachment; the canonical pattern is a sibling call immediately after
   init (e.g. `framework/EAs/QM5_41139_wti-mdaily-hl-mom/QM5_41139_wti-mdaily-hl-mom.mq5:914`).
   Combined with the no-arg `QM_IsNewBar()` at mq5:242 (chart-period clock while every
   indicator is pinned to `PERIOD_D1`), a wrong-period attachment re-evaluates the same D1
   breakout at unrelated chart-bar boundaries instead of failing closed.

2. **Risk management is gated behind entry eligibility** — `OnTick` returns on
   `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` (mq5:213) *before* `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` (mq5:216).
   `Strategy_NoTradeFilter` (mq5:51-66) rejects on warmup, zero/invalid quote, and
   wide-spread-vs-ATR. Under any of those, the card's ratcheting 2×ATR chandelier trail and
   the 30-trading-day time stop (both inside `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition`) are suspended on
   an already-open position. Open-position management and Friday close must be reachable
   independently of new-entry filters.

   nowhere (grep: single hit = the declaration). The card names a distinct 5×ATR
   catastrophic backstop (card "Stop Loss" section); the EA sets both initial SL and trail
   from `strategy_trail_atr_mult` (2×ATR) only. An advertised, sweepable risk knob with zero
   use-site is a dead input — either wire the backstop or remove the input via an approved
   card/input revision.

4. **Durable SPEC contradicts card + code** — `SPEC.md:64` declares base timeframe `H1`
   while card and every strategy read are D1; `SPEC.md:28` states "no strategy-specific
   inputs" while the MQ5 exposes 8 (`strategy_atr_period` … `strategy_warmup_bars`). SPEC is
   the durable downstream execution surface; regenerate it to D1 with the 8 governed inputs.
