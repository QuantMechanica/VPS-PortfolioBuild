## Rejecting findings

### R1 — The implemented entry mechanism is not the approved pending-stop mechanism

The card requires a `BUY_STOP` at `Buy_Trigger` and a `SELL_STOP` at `Sell_Trigger`, placed on the daily open (`approved card:90-94`). It defines each trigger from the current daily `Open_t` (`approved card:76-77`).

The EA instead:

- builds the reference from `CopyRates(..., shift=1, ...)` and uses `rates[0].open`, which is the previous completed D1 bar's open, not the current daily open (`QM5_37002_dual-thrust-asymmetric-range-breakout.mq5:81-83,108-112`);
- waits for `Close[1]` to be above/below those levels (`...mq5:167-186`);
- sets `req.price=0` and submits `QM_BUY`/`QM_SELL`, i.e. a market deal rather than the two card-required stop orders (`...mq5:152-158,176-186,337-342`; framework pending/deal selection is `framework/include/QM/QM_Entry.mqh:452-462`).

**Named fix:** rebuild the entry state machine through the governed V5 pending-order path: compute the levels from the current D1 open plus the closed-bar range, place card-faithful `BUY_STOP`/`SELL_STOP` orders at the trigger prices on daily open, and govern cancellation/fill ownership without substituting a close-confirmed market entry.

### R2 — Stop and exit mechanics are replaced by unapproved mechanics

The card requires the stop at the opposite trigger boundary (or the explicitly allowed entry +/- `0.50 * Range`) and a market EOD/settlement exit (`approved card:96-98`).

The EA instead installs a `2.0 * ATR(14)` stop (`...mq5:180,186`) and exits only on a close beyond the opposite trigger or after an invented five-D1-bar maximum hold (`...mq5:229-247`). No EOD/settlement exit exists. `strategy_sl_atr_mult` and `strategy_max_hold_bars` therefore change the strategy rather than parameterize an approved rule (`...mq5:53-54`).

**Named fix:** choose and document one of the card-authorized stop formulas, implement the card's EOD settlement close, and remove the opposite-close/max-hold exit unless an OWNER-approved card revision explicitly authorizes it.

### R3 — Card risk rails and execution tolerance are not wired

The approved card declares a 2.0% daily realized-loss entry halt, a 2.5% daily drawdown hard stop, a 5.0% total drawdown stop, and maximum slippage of 3 ticks (`approved card:83-88,110-113`). The EA exposes/wires none of those values. The framework call used by this EA initializes the generic daily kill switch at 3.0% and entry deviation at 20 points (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:296-298`), so those defaults are not evidence of card fidelity.

**Named fix:** expose and wire the three distinct card rails through governed entry-halt/kill-switch facilities, and bind the card's 3-tick execution tolerance through the framework entry configuration with correct tick-to-point conversion.

### R4 — Entry-only filters currently block position management and exits

`Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` returns before `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` and `Strategy_ExitSignal()` (`...mq5:305-310`). A rollover or spread-filter event can therefore suppress management and any strategy exit for an already-open position.

**Named fix:** keep MAE, kill switch, Friday close, management, and exits reachable for owned exposure; apply rollover/spread/daily-entry-halt/news gates only to new entries as their contracts require.
