## Defects (RECYCLE — actionable)

1. **[High] Exit suppressed by entry-eligibility filter.** OnTick returns on
   `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` (mq5:163) *before* `Strategy_ExitSignal()` (mq5:168).
   NoTradeFilter contains warmup + quote + spread gates (mq5:52,57,60-61) that are
   new-entry conditions. A bar whose spread exceeds `strategy_spread_max_atr*ATR`
   therefore skips the card's mandatory reverse-cross close of an open long.
   Fix: make Friday-close and the reverse-cross exit reachable independently of
   entry eligibility; apply the spread gate to entry construction only.

2. **[High] Symbol universe exceeds the approved card (ROT boundary).** The card
   ports the SPY source to `SP500.DWX` (backtest) + `NDX.DWX`/`WS30.DWX`
   (live-routable validation) only (docs/strategy_card.md R3). SPEC.md and the 13
   generated setfiles expand this to 13 symbols (adds GDAXI, UK100, XAUUSD, 7 FX)
   with no approved card revision. Card-universe expansion is a ROT decision.
   Fix: restrict sets to the approved 3-symbol port, or obtain an OWNER-approved
   card revision motivating the wider universe. (Magic rows exist for all 13 but
   registry identity does not authorize a market universe.)

3. **[Medium] Malformed setfile line endings.** All 13 sets contain 29 `0D 0D 0A`
   (CR CR LF) sequences each (verified SP500.DWX set: 58 total 0x0D). Visible risk
   values are correct but the doubled-CR records are not byte-stable for
   line-oriented tooling. Fix: regenerate through the governed generator
   (gen_setfile.ps1) with correct newline handling and refresh build_identity.
