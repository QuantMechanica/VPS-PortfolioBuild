### 1. Card-faithfulness defects

1. **Pending SELL-STOP replaced by market-on-close entry.** The card requires an
   expiring SELL-STOP at the ATR-buffered neckline and says it remains valid for 12 H4
   bars (`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_12931_classical-triple-top-reversal-h4.md:90-93`).
   The source instead waits for a closed-bar cross (`.mq5:426-430`), assigns
   `req.type = QM_SELL` and the current bid (`.mq5:447-448`), then calls the market
   entry helper (`.mq5:602`). There is no pending-order type or expiry. The mandated
   hardening check reports `EA_CARD_PENDING_ORDER_TYPE_MISMATCH`.

2. **Mandatory peak-significance gate is absent.** The card requires every peak to be
   at least `0.5 * ATR` above its surrounding 20-bar high (card `:60`). The controlling
   input `strategy_peak_height_atr` is declared at `.mq5:46` and has no executable
   use-site. `Strategy_FindFractals` only applies the configurable fractal wing test
   (`.mq5:217-263`). This omits a declared structural gate.

3. **The lower lookback bound is also dead.** The card declares a `[60, 200]` H4-bar
   lookback (card `:50-51`), but `strategy_lookback_min_bars` appears only in its
   declaration (`.mq5:42`). Detection always scans the maximum (`.mq5:295-301`).

4. **The exactly-one-trough rule is not enforced.** The card requires exactly one
   significant pivot low between each adjacent peak pair (card `:69-70`). The source
   accepts any number and merely selects the lowest candidate from each interval
   (`.mq5:345-375`).

5. **D1 SMA direction is inverted.** `CopyBuffer(start=1,count=2)` at `.mq5:182`
   places older shift 2 at index 0 and newer shift 1 at index 1. The comparison at
   `.mq5:187` therefore admits a rising SMA, not the card-required flat-or-falling D1
   SMA (`card:123-125`). Hardening reports `EA_CARD_SMA_DIRECTION_INVERTED`.

6. **News duration is 30/30 minutes instead of two H4 bars.** The card requires an
   entry pause within +/-2 H4 bars (`card:119`), i.e. 480/480 minutes. The source
   defaults to `QM_NEWS_TEMPORAL_PRE30_POST30` (`.mq5:25`) and also passes literal
   `30, 30` to framework initialization (`.mq5:545-548`). Hardening reports
   `EA_CARD_NEWS_WINDOW_MISMATCH`.

7. **Entry-only news gating blocks protective management and exits.** The news return
   is at `.mq5:570-575`, before partial management (`.mq5:580`) and the strategy exit
   block (`.mq5:582-590`). High-impact news can therefore suppress the card's partial,
   break-even, false-breakdown, and time-stop handling. Hardening reports
   `EA_CARD_NEWS_BLOCKS_MANAGEMENT`.

8. **Partial-close failure is treated as success.** The bool result from
   `QM_TM_PartialClose` is discarded at `.mq5:490`, after which `g_tp1_done` is set
   unconditionally at `.mq5:492`. A rejected/unrepresentable partial close permanently
   suppresses the card's 50% partial behavior.

9. **Open-position management state is not restart-safe.** The neckline, TP1, and
   completion flags are file-scope state initialized to empty (`.mq5:72-77`) and are not
   reconstructed in `OnInit` (`.mq5:543-555`). After a restart with an owned position,
   `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition` exits at `.mq5:474-475`, and the first-eight-bar
   pattern-failure exit is disabled by the same flag at `.mq5:517`.

These are salvageable implementation defects, so the disposition is RECYCLE rather
than FAILED.
