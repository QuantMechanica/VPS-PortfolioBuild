- Setup: completed D1 RSI(8) above 70 for long or below 30 for short.
- Entry event: one buy stop 20 pips above the current D1 open or one sell stop
  20 pips below it. The framework pip helper handles JPY and non-JPY quoting.
- The approved card permits bar-0 live RSI or bar-1 confirmed RSI. The build
  uses shift 1 so decisions are reproducible from completed data.
- If price passed the stop level before the first executable tick, the order is
  skipped rather than converted into an unauthorized market entry.
- Pending lifetime: current D1 bar only, with broker expiration and stale-order
  cleanup at the next bar.
- Stop and target: 2 and 4 times completed-bar ATR(14), respectively, measured
  from the pending fill.
- Fixed strategy parameters fail closed because the card supplies no approved
  P3 search range. Symbol, slot, EA ID, and timeframe also fail closed.

  corrects the old partial file, which defaulted all requests to slot 0.
- MAE sampling occurs before per-tick guards. News gates entries only; Friday
  close and management remain ahead of news. Indicator reads occur only behind
  the single `QM_IsNewBar()` gate.
- One position and pending order per magic; no grid, martingale, adaptive-PnL
  logic, stop mutation, or ML.
