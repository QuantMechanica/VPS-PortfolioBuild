## Implemented mechanic

- `BB(20,1.0)` defines the inner range boundary; `BB(20,2.0)` caps the trend
  zone.
- Default state requires six consecutive completed H4 bars inside the inner
  envelope (shifts 2 through 7).
- The completed trigger bar at shift 1 must close between the inner and outer
  upper bands for a long, or between the outer and inner lower bands for a
  short.
- Entry is at the next H4 open with a stop 15 pips beyond the crossed inner-band
  boundary and no fixed take-profit.
- A completed H4 close back through the relevant inner boundary exits the
  position.
- Indicator reads and signal/exit state advance only after the central
  `QM_IsNewBar()` gate.
- Configuration is fail-closed for the exact ten symbol/slot pairs and H4.
- The central and custom news gates are entry-only; Friday close, position
  management, and the strategy exit remain operative.
- One position per symbol/magic; no grid, martingale, ML, adaptive-PnL logic, or
  non-framework indicator handle is present.
