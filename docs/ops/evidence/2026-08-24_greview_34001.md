### 3. Card-invented Kalman and TP mechanics

- The card defines the Kalman recurrence at card lines 74-78 but does not define a finite window, initial state, or initial covariance. MQ5 lines 61-68 invent `lookback=100`, initialize `x` from the oldest close, initialize `p=1.0`, and recompute the filter from scratch. These choices materially determine the Z-score and entries.
- The card requires TP at the Kalman state estimate at card line 98. MQ5 lines 162-165 and 177-180 silently replace an invalid/wrong-side estimate with a target `1.5 * sl_dist`, an unapproved exit model.

**Repair:** obtain an approved card rule for filter initialization/warm-up/state persistence; implement that exact rule. Reject an entry when the exact Kalman TP is invalid, or obtain an approved fallback rule—do not invent an ATR target.

### 4. Protective paths are behind entry-only gates

MQ5 lines 223-231 return on the central news filter and the strategy no-trade filter before `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` at line 233 and `Strategy_ExitSignal()` at line 235. The news return also precedes the mandatory Friday-close sweep at line 230. A news blackout can therefore suppress Friday liquidation, and entry-only rollover/spread filters suppress management/exit paths.

The card also depicts protected break-even and trailing states at lines 142-153, but `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` is empty and `Strategy_ExitSignal()` always returns false at MQ5 lines 189-196. Since the card gives no numeric triggers, Development must not guess them; Research/OWNER must specify them or explicitly mark that diagram non-operative.

**Repair:** place Friday close and protective management/exit paths before entry-only gates, following the canonical framework template; resolve the card ambiguity for break-even/trailing before implementing either.

### 5. Raw-series calls have no governed performance allowance

MQ5 lines 142-143 call `iClose` and `iOpen` directly. The EA contains no `PERF_ALLOW*` annotation. This violates the review contract's “no raw series without perf-allowed annotation” rule. The bounded `CopyRates(..., lookback=100)` path at lines 61-65 has a returned-count guard before indexing, but the two raw calls bypass it.

**Repair:** reuse a bounded rates buffer with returned-count/`ArraySize` guards, or add the exact governed performance allowance if the raw calls are explicitly approved.
