### False negatives versus cutoff

**Cut means percent KEPT**, not percent discarded. Pilot reproduction uses `k=floor(n*keep)` (minimum one), matching the narrative's 9/19 top-half denominator; ties resolve by year then cell key. FN@k = members of the real-score top-k omitted by the cheap-score top-k divided by k. This is loss of a ranking target, not loss of a profitable/admissible strategy. Pooled cell rankings are diagnostic only: a year's market regime must never decide which other year's configuration survives.

| Mode / scope | Keep 30%: FN@k | Keep 50%: FN@k | Keep 70%: FN@k |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHLC-M1 pooled n=19 | 2/5 = 40% | 0/9 = 0% | 0/13 = 0% |
| OHLC-M1 2021 n=10 | 0/3 = 0% | 1/5 = 20% | 0/7 = 0% |
| Open-prices 2021 n=10 | 0/3 = 0% | 1/5 = 20% | 0/7 = 0% |
| Gross real cells saved / 100, after 10% dropped controls | 63 | 45 | 27 |

Because the target k changes with the cutoff, FN@k is not necessarily monotonic. Holding the true top-half target fixed avoids the misleading impression that keep 30% is safer than keep 50%: pooled OHLC-M1 misses **4/9, 0/9, 0/9**; both 2021 modes miss **2/5, 1/5, 0/5**, at keep 30/50/70 respectively. At keep 50%, OHLC-M1 loses 2021 `s1_l8`; open-prices loses `s2_l6`. The pooled keep-30% cut loses both of these 2021 cells.

Descriptive 95% Wilson intervals illustrate the limited denominators: 0/9 → [0%,29.9%], 1/5 → [3.6%,62.4%], 0/7 → [0%,35.4%], 2/5 → [11.8%,76.9%]. They are not calibrated population guarantees for dependent top-k membership. Zero observed misses does **not** show FN≤10%. The primary scopes have no constant bootstrap resamples. Repeated-year agreement does not establish preservation of the plateau winner or the walk-forward selection; both remain **NICHT GEZEIGT**.

## Savings, backlog and Q14 impact

Whole-year bundles preserve the same proportion only when their costs and sizes match. For implementation, controls are rounded upward per cohort; baselines, neighbours, rescued false negatives, repeat runs and periodic full-truth cohorts reduce savings.
