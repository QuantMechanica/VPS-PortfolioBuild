## Findings requiring recycle

### 1. High — the 2% realized-loss halt measures floating equity

The card requires a daily **realized** loss entry halt at 2.0%
(`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout.md:87`).
`StrategyDailyEntryHalt` instead compares current `ACCOUNT_EQUITY` with
`g_qm_ks_day_start_equity`
(`framework/EAs/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout.mq5:77`).
That includes open P&L and collapses the card's realized-loss entry control
into the separate equity drawdown control.

Named fix: compute UTC-day closed-deal net P&L from broker history (including
the broker-reported profit, swap, commission, and fee fields), derive the
day-start balance, and fail closed when history selection is unavailable.
Leave floating-equity enforcement to the existing 2.5% kill switch.

### 2. High — the mandatory 16:00 UTC close deasserts at 23:55

The card requires force-close at 16:00 GMT
(`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout.md:99`).
The source returns true only while `hhmm_utc >= strategy_force_close_hhmm &&
hhmm_utc < 2355`
(`framework/EAs/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout/QM5_35004_babypips-asian-box-london-breakout.mq5:327`).
After a quote gap or failed close, the required exit therefore becomes false
at 23:55 and remains false through midnight.

Named fix: anchor the 16:00 UTC deadline to each owned position's entry UTC
date and keep the exit asserted until the position is confirmed closed.

Named fix: remove the unapproved break-even logic and retain the broker-side
