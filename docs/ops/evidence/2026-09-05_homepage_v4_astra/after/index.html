The mechanics we model

Different hypotheses.
The same demand for evidence.

01 / SESSION STRUCTURE

Liquidity around the handoff.

ICT / SMT session models examine how related markets take liquidity around a session change. The hypothesis is relative confirmation, followed by a mechanically defined response.

02 / INDICATOR REGIME

When direction becomes persistent.

Regime models ask whether a market is trending or rotating. Indicators describe that state; the strategy still needs a repeatable entry, exit and cost-aware test.

03 / CALENDAR & RELATIVE VALUE

A different rhythm near the calendar turn.

Calendar models investigate recurring settlement and month-end behavior. Relative-value versions compare related exposures to test whether the pattern persists beyond one market.
