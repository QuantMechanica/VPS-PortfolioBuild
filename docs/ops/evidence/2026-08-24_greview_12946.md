### Blocking finding: exit detection is behind the news-entry gate

The EA calculates the adverse MACD cross and opposite divergence only inside
`Strategy_EntrySignal` at
`framework/EAs/QM5_12946_mql5-macd-obv-div-card/QM5_12946_mql5-macd-obv-div-card.mq5:277-302`,
setting `g_exit_cached` at `:295-302`. In `OnTick`, the cached exit is consumed
at `:448-462`, but the news check can return at `:464-474` before the new-bar
gate at `:476-477` and before the only call to `Strategy_EntrySignal` at
`:481-487`.

Thus management is superficially placed before the news gate, but the facts
that cause the card's early exit are not computed there. During a blackout the
hard SL and TP remain active, but the explicitly promised early-exit overlay is
delayed. This is a repairable source defect, hence `RECYCLE`, not `FAILED`.
