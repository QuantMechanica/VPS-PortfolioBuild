`framework/EAs/QM5_12946_mql5-macd-obv-div-card/QM5_12946_mql5-macd-obv-div-card.mq5`
- `g_exit_cached` is SET only inside `Strategy_EntrySignal` (lines 298, 302).
- In OnTick, `Strategy_EntrySignal` is called at line 483 — AFTER the news-allows return (line 474)
  and the QM_IsNewBar return (line 477). The exit is CONSUMED at line 450 (`Strategy_ExitSignal`),
  correctly before the news gate.
- Consequence: an opposite confirmed divergence or adverse MACD zero-line cross that occurs
  DURING a news blackout is not detected/cached until the blackout clears and a new bar arrives.
  This violates the framework corset that exits/management are news-independent.
- Actionable fix: move the exit-condition detection (lines ~289-302) out of the news-gated
  `Strategy_EntrySignal` into `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` (runs every tick before the news
  gate) or into `Strategy_ExitSignal` itself; keep closed-bar semantics via an internal
  QM_IsNewBar guard, but do not leave detection behind the OnTick news-allows return.
- Impact bound: minor — exit is a "close early" overlay on a hard SL + 2R TP; delayed detection
  is ~1 H1 bar per news window. Not INFRA-breaking, but a genuine, cheap-to-fix conformity defect.
