## Actionable RECYCLE findings

### 1. Card-mandated three-tick market-order slippage is not wired

The approved card fixes market-order slippage at a maximum of three ticks (`D:/QM/strategy_farm/artifacts/cards_approved/QM5_41001_keith-fitschen-aberration-trading-system.md:113`). The EA calls `QM_FrameworkInit` but never overrides the entry deviation (`QM5_41001_keith-fitschen-aberration-trading-system.mq5:273-288`). The framework consequently configures `QM_Entry` with its generic 20-point deviation (`framework/include/QM/QM_Common.mqh:296-297`), which is copied to every trade request (`framework/include/QM/QM_Entry.mqh:462`). A point allowance is not the card's symbol-specific three-trade-tick allowance.

Named fix: after successful `QM_FrameworkInit`, fail closed if `SYMBOL_POINT` or `SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE` is unavailable; convert `3 * SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE` to points without rounding upward and call `QM_EntryConfigure` with that deviation and the existing news/stress/magic values. Add a focused static regression test.
