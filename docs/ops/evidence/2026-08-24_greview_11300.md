## Blocking findings

### 1. The card's partial-exit mechanism is not implemented

The approved card requires three one-third exits at 1x, 2x, and 3x ATR (card lines 69-75), and
its implementation notes explicitly prescribe three separate P2 orders with one-third lot each
(card lines 122-124). The EA instead declares one configurable TP multiple
(`.mq5:43`), calculates one TP at that multiple (`.mq5:152,160`), and sends one entry request
(`.mq5:275-280`). There is no partial-close or three-order implementation. This changes payoff
shape and exit timing, so it is not a documentation-only deviation.

**Named fix:** implement the card's 1x/2x/3x ATR one-third exit schedule using the governed
one-position-per-magic-compatible design described by the card, with restart-safe tracking and
no new strategy degrees of freedom. Remove the invented single `strategy_tp_atr_mult` mechanic
unless the approved card is formally amended.

### 2. The required PSAR-flip close cannot occur

The card requires closing the remaining position on a PSAR reversal (card lines 70-75). The EA's
only explicit strategy exit always returns false (`.mq5:218-221`). Its management hook only moves
a long SL while the PSAR remains below bid and a short SL while PSAR remains above ask
(`.mq5:203-213`); once the dot flips to the opposite side, those conditions reject the new PSAR
and do not close the position. The resulting behavior is not equivalent to the card's explicit
flip-close rule.

**Named fix:** detect the closed-bar PSAR side flip and close the owned remainder through the
framework trade manager, preserving magic/symbol ownership and management-before-entry ordering.

### 3. A too-wide PSAR risk is converted into a trade instead of skipped

The card says to skip the entry when PSAR distance exceeds `1.5 * ATR` (card lines 76 and 89-93).
The EA instead replaces a too-wide or invalid-side PSAR stop with an ATR-clamped stop and proceeds
(`.mq5:146-160`). That admits trades the approved mechanism excludes and changes their initial
stop anchor.

**Named fix:** retain the card's PSAR initial stop and return no signal when its distance/side
fails the approved `1.5 * ATR` risk-distance condition. Do not silently substitute another stop.
