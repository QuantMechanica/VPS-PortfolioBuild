### F1 — The delivered universe is not the approved card universe

The approved card binds the strategy to the six-pair FX cohort `EURUSD.DWX`, `GBPUSD.DWX`, `USDJPY.DWX`, `AUDUSD.DWX`, `EURJPY.DWX`, and `GBPJPY.DWX` (`card:21`, `card:91`, `card:104-106`). The repaired SPEC instead declares four of those FX pairs plus five equity indices (`SPEC.md:40-49`), and the nine delivered setfiles follow that altered SPEC. There are no `EURJPY.DWX` or `GBPJPY.DWX` setfiles. The 2026-08-23 repair therefore made setfiles agree with a locally revised SPEC, not with the OWNER-approved card.

The registry compounds the mismatch: current rows for EA 12923 are at `framework/registry/magic_numbers.csv:16213-16225` and contain no EURJPY/GBPJPY slots. Existing delivered setfiles do use valid registered slots and the expected `ea_id * 10000 + slot` values, but registry validity does not authorize a different strategy universe.

**Named fix:** restore `SPEC.md` to the approved six-symbol FX cohort; allocate deterministic registry/magic rows for EURJPY and GBPJPY through the governed allocator; regenerate the six approved backtest setfiles through the governed generator; and remove the index cohort from this build unless an OWNER-authorized card revision explicitly approves it.

### F2 — The approved 25-point spread cap is absent

The card requires `Spread cap: 25 pts` (`card:76`). The complete entry path at `QM5_12923_hopwood-dmi-cross-h1-card.mq5:78-139` validates prices and point size but never tests current spread against 25 points. A source grep for `spread` returns no hit. This changes which Q02 trades are admitted and cannot be waived by a passing syntactic guardrail.

**Named fix:** add a card-bound strategy spread-cap input/default of 25 points, use it in an entry-only spread check (management and exits must remain reachable), include it in every governed setfile, and add a focused unit/static regression proving over-cap entries are rejected without blocking exits.

### F3 — P2/Q02 news filtering is active although the card says off by default

The card says the news hook is “off by default for P2” (`card:77`). The EA defaults to `QM_NEWS_TEMPORAL_PRE30_POST30` and `QM_NEWS_COMPLIANCE_DXZ` (`mq5:23-27`), while none of the nine backtest setfiles overrides those inputs. The entry path actively calls `QM_NewsAllowsTrade2` under those defaults (`mq5:253-259`). Thus the delivered Q02 presets test a news-filtered variant rather than the approved no-filter baseline.

**Named fix:** regenerate every approved Q02/backtest setfile with the governed OFF/NONE news configuration required by the card. Keep the framework news path wired so later governed live/news phases can enable it; do not disable position management or exits.
