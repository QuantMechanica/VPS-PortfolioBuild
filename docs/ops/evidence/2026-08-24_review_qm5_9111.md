## Actionable defects (fix, rebuild, re-review before Q02)

### D1 (High) — spread gate is not the card's rule
Card "Additional filters": *"Skip entries when spread exceeds 2.5 x 20-day median spread."*
The EA implements an ATR ratio instead:
- `QM5_9111_aa-dlwma-trend10.mq5:73` `SpreadAllows()` tests `spread <= strategy_spread_atr_mult * atr_val` with `strategy_spread_atr_mult=0.3` (`:51`).
ATR measures price range, not the historical spread distribution, so this admits/rejects a
different set of entries than the sealed card. `SpreadAllows()` also returns `true` when
`atr_val <= 0.0` (`:71`) — missing filter evidence becomes permission to trade (fail-open).
Required: compute a deterministic 20-day median spread, apply the fixed 2.5x multiplier to
entry admission only, fail closed on missing/invalid sample.

### D2 (High) — entry-only gates suppress card exits and risk reduction
In `OnTick()` the news gate and no-trade filter both `return` before `Strategy_ExitSignal()`:
- `:265` `if(!news_allows) return;`
- `:268` `if(Strategy_NoTradeFilter()) return;` — and `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` returns true on wide spread / min-bar shortfall (`:132`, spread via `SpreadAllows`).
Consequently a news blackout or a wide spread can delay the card's mandated opposite-zero-cross
exit. The news `return` also precedes Friday-close handling (`:267`). These are entry gates; the
card does not authorize them to disable position management. Required: run framework risk handling
and card exits independently of new-entry eligibility.
