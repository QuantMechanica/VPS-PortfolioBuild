## Actionable findings

### F1 — REJECT: Supertrend ATR period does not follow the card's explicit formula

The approved mechanics compute `atr = ATR(14)` and use it in both Supertrend bands
(card lines 47-53); the Build-EA Notes repeat `iATR(..., 14, ...)` (card lines
103-106). The source instead assigns `period = strategy_supertrend_period` (default
10) at `.mq5:98` and passes that value to `QM_ATR` at `.mq5:120`. The separate
`strategy_atr_period=14` input is used only for the catastrophic stop at
`.mq5:178-179`.

The card is internally ambiguous because its source discussion calls `(period=10,
multiplier=3.0)` the cardified pairing (card lines 37-38) and its P3 sweep includes a
`period` parameter (card line 75). That ambiguity does not authorize silently
departing from the card's closed-form ATR(14) mechanics and explicit build note.

**Named fix:** obtain the required approved-card reconciliation for what `period=10`
means, then implement the reconciled Supertrend ATR period exactly. If the current
approved formula remains controlling, feed ATR(14) to the Supertrend recurrence; do
not silently select ATR(10).

### F2 — REJECT: the 60-trading-bar stop is implemented as 60 calendar days

The card requires a time stop after 60 trading days / D1 bars (card lines 63-65).
The source compares elapsed seconds to
`strategy_max_hold_bars * PeriodSeconds(D1)` at `.mq5:242-244,259-262`. Weekends and
market holidays therefore count toward the threshold, so the position can close
materially before 60 completed D1 bars.

**Named fix:** count completed D1 trading bars since the position opened and trigger
the fallback only at the card's 60-bar boundary. Preserve the opposite-flip and
catastrophic-stop behavior.
