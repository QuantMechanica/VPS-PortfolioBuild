## Blocking findings

1. **Pending entries remain executable inside mandatory no-entry windows.**
   The EA submits broker-side stop orders with a 24-hour lifetime
   (`.mq5:179,201`). `OnTick` returns on the news gate (`:259-264`), Friday
   close (`:266`), and rollover/spread filter (`:267`) before pending-order
   management (`:275`). The only cancellation paths are the next D1 entry
   evaluation (`:135`) or after a position has filled (`:215-219`). An order
   submitted before a blackout can therefore fill during the blackout. This
   reproduces the router's prior blocking finding.

2. **Card-declared loss rails are absent.** The card requires a 2.0% daily
   realized-loss entry halt (`card:86`), a 2.5% maximum daily drawdown stop, and
   a 5.0% total drawdown stop (`card:110-111`). The EA declares none of these
   and calls legacy `QM_FrameworkInit` (`.mq5:236-242`), which initializes the
   framework kill switch to 3.0% daily and 0.0% total
   (`QM_Common.mqh:298`). Canonical `build_gate_hardening.py` reports three
   `EA_CARD_LOSS_LIMIT_MISMATCH` failures.

3. **The card's GMT rollover contract is evaluated in broker time.** The card
   says 23:55–00:05 GMT (`card:85`), while `Strategy_NoTradeFilter` reads raw
   `TimeCurrent()` (`.mq5:100-105`) without `QM_BrokerToUTC`.
   `build_gate_hardening.py` reports
   `EA_BROKER_TIME_USED_FOR_GMT_WINDOW`.

4. **Pending-order deletion bypasses the framework.** The EA includes
   `Trade/Trade.mqh` and invokes `CTrade.OrderDelete` directly (`.mq5:7,71-72`)
   instead of the governed `QM_TM_RemovePendingOrder` path.

5. **The source default risk contract fails closed.** Both
   `RISK_PERCENT=0.5` and `RISK_FIXED=1000.0` are positive (`.mq5:19-20`), a
   combination rejected by `QM_FrameworkValidateRiskInputs`. The governed
   backtest sets correctly override this to `RISK_FIXED=1000` and
   `RISK_PERCENT=0`, but a default/live attach has no valid percent-risk mode.

6. **Tick and entry mechanics are not frozen card mechanics.** The card's
   offsets are ticks, but the source substitutes `SYMBOL_POINT` (`.mq5:140,160`)
   rather than `SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE`, and the fixed two-tick stop is coupled
   to the configurable entry buffer (`:169,191`). It also adds undocumented
   next-quote gap checks (`:171,193`) and a 24-hour expiry. The approved card
   needs clarification for its internally contradictory lookback expression:
   it includes bar `[1]` in the 20-bar extreme while also requiring bar `[1]`
   to breach that extreme; the EA silently resolves this as bars `[2..21]`
   (`:146-147`).
