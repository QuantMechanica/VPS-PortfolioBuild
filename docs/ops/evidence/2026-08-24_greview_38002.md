The EA instead declares `strategy_atr_sl_mult = 1.5` (`mq5:42-44`) and places the stop at `ATR * strategy_atr_sl_mult` from entry (`mq5:150-157`). 3. **GMT rollover is evaluated in raw broker time.** The card specifies `23:55` through `00:05` GMT (`card:83-87`). `StrategyInRolloverWindow()` extracts hours from its input (`mq5:59-71`) and is called with `TimeCurrent()` (`mq5:108-111`) without `QM_BrokerToUTC` or an approved equivalent. 4. **Entry filtering still gates active-position management, and the spread gate is evaluated from stale/uninitialized state.** `OnTick()` returns on `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()` before calling `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition()` (`mq5:243-246`), so an open trade is not trailed during the rollover window or when the spread filter is active even though the approved lifecycle requires ATR trailing (`card:96-99`, `card:135-153`).
