## Findings (all persist; file:line)

1. **Card-faithfulness (design gap, OWNER note):** card frames a cointegrated-pair spread
   ("BUY/SELL Spread Package", §3.2/3.3), but `ComputeOrnsteinUhlenbeck` (lines 76-139) fits OU
   on a single instrument's raw non-stationary close and `Strategy_EntrySignal` (lines 175-224)
   sends one order on `_Symbol`. No second leg, hedge ratio, or spread construction. Single-symbol
   is fine for live per convention; the mean-reversion premise on a raw FX level is weaker than the
   card narrative. Flag for OWNER — not a blocking code defect on its own.
2. **Correctness — exit runs on every tick with full-window regression:** OnTick calls
   `Strategy_ExitSignal()` (line 339) *before* the `QM_IsNewBar()` gate (line 361); ExitSignal
   recomputes the 100-bar OLS (`CopyRates(...,101)`, line 89 via line 253) every tick while a
   position is open. Defeats closed-bar caching — the per-tick full-window perf class.
3. **Correctness — time stop ignores fitted half-life:** card §3.4 = `2.5·τ`; line 274 uses
   `strategy_time_stop_mult * strategy_max_half_life` (constant 40), giving every trade the same
   ~100-bar stop regardless of its fitted τ. Entry τ is never persisted per position.
4. **Correctness — protective exits suspended by entry filters:** `Strategy_NoTradeFilter()`
   (line 334) returns before `Strategy_ExitSignal()` (line 339), so during rollover blackout or a
   spread spike an open position cannot exit — risk trapped in exactly the volatile windows.
5. **Card contract — drawdown controls absent:** card §3.1.3 (2.0% daily-loss entry halt) and §4.2
   (2.5% daily, 5.0% total DD) are not wired; `Strategy_NoTradeFilter` only checks rollover+spread.
   Generic `QM_KillSwitchInit` defaults apply, not card numbers. (Systemic build-lane gap, also seen
   in the NNFX family.)
