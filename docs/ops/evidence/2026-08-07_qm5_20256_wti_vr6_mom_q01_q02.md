## Edge and non-duplication

On the first processed D1 bar of a genuine new broker month, the EA derives
32 chronological WTI monthly log returns from 33 consecutive completed month
ends. It combines the latest six-month return with a heteroskedasticity-robust
`q=7` variance-ratio state over lags one through six. Significant persistence
continues the R6 direction; significant anti-persistence reverses it; an
insignificant or invalid state consumes the month flat. A position has one
frozen `3.5*ATR(20,D1)` stop and exits on the next broker month or after 35
calendar days.

The deterministic pre-allocation check found no exact collision across 4,313
EA registry rows and 430 cards. Manual review resolved the expected family
neighbors: `QM5_13134` is R1-q2, `QM5_20253` is R3-q4, and
`QM5_20059` is plain six-month momentum without the six-lag memory statistic,
significance gate, anti-persistence reversal, or flat state. The R6 horizon,
q7 lag/weight vector, robust weights, and resulting direction path are jointly
load-bearing and not implemented by an existing EA.
