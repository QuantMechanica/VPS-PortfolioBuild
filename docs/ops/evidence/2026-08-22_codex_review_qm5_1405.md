## Blocking findings

1. **Required source data is absent and silently replaced with invented proxies.** The card requires Treasury yields, short-term Treasury yield, investment-grade corporate-bond yield, long-term Treasury yield, and a four-month-lagged S&P 500 earnings yield. It explicitly records R3 as `UNKNOWN` because approved external/custom-symbol handling is still required. The candidate reads only the chart symbol's D1 closes and a 200-day SMA, then fabricates `earnings_yield_proxy`, `t_yield_proxy`, and `corp_yield_proxy` from price returns and constants (MQ5 lines 129-171). These formulas do not appear in the approved card or cited strategy and produce a different signal.

2. **The four-month earnings-data lag is not implemented.** `strategy_earnings_lag_months` is declared but never used (MQ5 line 41). Because no earnings series is consumed at all, the card's no-lookahead publication-lag requirement cannot be satisfied.

3. **The Best Value allocation rule was reduced to equity-or-cash.** The card says allocate 100% to the corresponding winning asset when the highest normalized Treasury, corporate-bond, or equity premium is positive. The candidate sets `allocate_equity=false` whenever Treasury or corporate credit wins and therefore treats every non-equity winner as cash (MQ5 lines 199-207). No approved proxy sleeve selection or cross-symbol allocation exists.

4. **The symbol universe is unauthorized.** The card targets only SP500.DWX, NDX.DWX, and WS30.DWX. The build registers and emits setfiles for ten additional symbols, including FX, European indices, and gold. Running the same-symbol fabricated price model on EURUSD or XAUUSD does not turn those instruments into the card's US equity sleeve or macro input series.

5. **The normalization history is not expanding.** The card requires expanding historical means and standard deviations using all data available up to each month. The implementation caps the sample at 120 observations and constructs all observations from the current chart instrument (MQ5 lines 132-201). This changes the model even aside from the invalid inputs.

6. **The advertised median-spread filter computes a mean.** Both the parameter/spec call this a rolling median, but `Strategy_SpreadAllowsEntry` sums samples and divides by count (MQ5 lines 103-126). This is an internal spec/code mismatch and changes tail-spread behavior.

7. **The approved card is not build-ready on its own evidence.** Although its front matter says G0 APPROVED, the card body marks `card_body_incomplete: true` and R3 `UNKNOWN`, specifically because the external macro series and tradable bond/custom-symbol mappings are unresolved. A safe implementation must hold for approved deterministic input lineage; it must not manufacture substitutes to force a compilable EA.
