## QM5_20150 / STR-137

Approved because the card preserves the reconciled source test object:

- H4 EMA20/50 on close and Stochastic(14,3,1, SMA, CLOSE/CLOSE).
- A bull EMA cross arms only when K is at least 80 on that same closed bar;
  the bear mirror requires K at most 20.
- The arm first requires the opposite stochastic extreme, then the first K/D
  line hook at that boundary. An EMA recross cancels the arm and the first
  hook consumes the setup whether entry is accepted or rejected.
- The impulse start is the extreme of the complete preceding opposite-EMA
  regime including the cross bar. The walk is capped at 500 bars and fails
  closed with a logged event when the boundary cannot be established.
- The impulse end is the directional extreme from cross through hook. There
  is no TP.
- The close-qualified Fibonacci ladder ratchets a real server stop
  monotonically: F(1) to entry, each later reached level to its predecessor,
  and a final cap at F(4.618).
- A completed opposite setup closes next bar and is reserved for exit so it
  cannot reverse the position on the same bar.

### Required exact-recursion audit

The incoming card referred to “codex 02 §3” but did not fully transcribe the
strategy-defining recursion. The approved card now states it directly:

1. `TR=max(H-L,abs(H-Cprev),abs(L-Cprev))`.
2. Seed Wilder ATR(7) with the arithmetic mean of the first seven true ranges;
   thereafter `ATR=(6*ATRprev+TR)/7`.
3. For each multiplier 0.9 and 1.8, calculate midpoint basic bands. Replace
   final upper only when basic upper is lower than prior final upper or prior
   close is above that prior band; otherwise carry it. Apply the exact mirrored
   rule to final lower.
4. If the prior line was final upper, remain DOWN when current close is at or
   below current final upper, else flip UP to current final lower. If the prior
   line was final lower, remain UP when current close is at or above current
   final lower, else flip DOWN to current final upper.
5. At the first ATR-ready bar, equality to midpoint seeds UP/final-lower;
   otherwise seed DOWN/final-upper.

The hook implements those rules oldest-to-newest in strategy-owned closed-H1
arrays. It rebuilds a bounded 300-bar state once per forming H1 bar with
sanctioned `QM_ReadBar` records, uses no `iCustom`, and reads pooled EMA, RSI,
and ADX buffers only at shifts 1 and 2.

An independent hand fixture passed: seven true ranges of 2 seed ATR at 2.0;
the next equal-range bar carries the upper bands and advances the lower bands;
a following gap-down true range of 5 produces ATR `17/7`, flips the fast line
DOWN, and leaves the slow line UP. This covers the mean seed, Wilder update,
band carry, direction recursion, and the deliberate multiplier divergence.

## QM5_20152 / STR-143

Approved because the card and hook preserve the conservative reconciled
baseline:

- The only baseline symbol is `EURUSD.DWX` on H1, matching the source's single
  illustrated market. Multi-pair testing remains a separately labeled variant.
- SMA100/200 crossover arms an episode. The first strictly later closed bar
  whose LOWHIGH Stochastic(14,3,3) K line crosses out through 25/75 triggers;
  a K/D cross is not substituted.
- A stochastic cross on the SMA-cross bar cannot enter. Equality after arming
  cancels until a fresh SMA cross, and a qualifying trigger consumes the
  episode even when a common gate rejects entry.
- Initial server protection is 150 pips and the target is 300 pips from actual
  fill. A post-fill correction may tighten but never widen; if exact protection
  would require widening, the implementation fails closed by flattening with
  per-H1 retry pacing.
- A completed bar reaching +150 pips latches BE. The next-bar stop move is
  tighten-only and once-latched; the signal bar cannot satisfy the post-entry
  test. Restart detection recognizes an already-at-BE stop and never widens it.

The LOWHIGH stochastic uses the pooled `QM_Stoch_K` reader directly. A focused
episode fixture confirmed that a boundary move on the SMA-cross bar is blocked,
the first later 25-level exit triggers once, and a later move in the same
episode remains consumed.
