on your first lot and 70 pips on your second lot for a total of 110 pips versus up to 30 pips risk (15 pips + 15 pips). That’s nearly

So, does the J16 enter 2 orders or just one that gets closed out partially and then continues to run?
J16 is a trade manager. You have to manually open the pending orders yourself and set the final Take profit. Then place J16 on
the chart and set the Break even activate to 30 or 40 Breakeven exit pips to 0 or 1 and Partial close activate to 40 and partial
close percent at 0.5 (50%)
It will then move your stop loss to break even at 30 pips or 40 pips to break even or break even +1 and take 50% of the trade off
the board at 40 pips. I set the break even activate to 30 and the partial close activate to 40. You can set them at 30 and 30 or 40
and 40 or whatever. So you only have to open one order but double the lots you would if you were pening two orders. That is,…
If you are trading 1 lot per trade you would open one order instead for two lots. It will close out one lot automatcially at 40 pips.
Edit: You will also have to set your stop loss manually too. the intial stop loss of 15 pips+spread.
