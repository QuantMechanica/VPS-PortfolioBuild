Hi all,
I will introduce my self first,
I have been trading stock market for over 3 year and start trading forex for 1 year.
This method is not a new method that i create, i just improve from others trading systems to suit my own trading
style.
I'm not a profession trader, I still in a learning process.
My Strategy is using H1 Time Frame, SMA 50 and Horizontal Line rounded number 25 pip (000, 250, 500, 750).
Rules of Entry:
Long Position:
- Price above SMA50,
- Pending buy order at the Horizontal Line above the SMA50 (000 / 250 / 500 / 750), Usually give 3 pip above the
horizontal.
- Target 50 Pip, SL 30 Pip (R:R = 1 : 1.5)
Short Position:
- Price Below SMA50
- Pending Sell order at the Horizontal Line below the SMA50 (000 / 250 / 500 / 750), Usually give 3 pip below the
horizontal.
- Target 50 Pip, SL 30 Pip (R:R = 1 : 1.5)
more rule:
- I don't take the trade when the horizontal line is to close to the MA50
- Move the SL to BE+1 / BE+5 when the price goes to your TP direction (after 10-15 pip)
