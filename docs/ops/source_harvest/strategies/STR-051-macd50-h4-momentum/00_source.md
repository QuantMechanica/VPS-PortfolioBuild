loss level.

the past.

Entries

Entry 0
Basic entries for this system is when the previous 3 MACD signals has a difference of + 50 (long) or -50 (Short)
Explanation of 3 previous Macd Signals.
Example: time is 08:00 a new macd signal has formed Now take the macd number from 20:00 and if there is
difference of +/-50 from the 04:00 signal go long/short at 08:00

Dean Simpson's Trading Plan

Routine
Monday to Friday arrive at the machine at 7.45.
Check on any previous positions from the previous Day
Calculate Appropriate lot sizes for the current Account Balance
Check the current MACD Signal on 240minute cable chart to see if its +/- 0.00050
difference from the 8pm signal
If The MACD difference is indicating to take a position and there are no trades open then
buy/sell 2 positions and set the correct SL and TP prices for them both
Repeat again at 11.45 then at 15:45 and finally at 20:45
Trade Strategies, Setups, Entries and exits
Mechanical MACD system
The intention of this system is to capture 75 pips from a 45 pip movement in the daily trend of
currency pairs. The system allows for entry and exits to be easily defined and to remove any
===== PAGE 20/33 =====
negative affects that my emotions could have on the outcome of the trade.
Settings
4 Hour chart
MACD (5,13,1)
Entries – Long And Short
At 8am, 12pm, 4pm and 8pm check the chart to see if there is a positive or negative difference
of 0.00050 Between the 3 previous MACD signals.
If no trades are currently open. And If there is a positive/negative difference of 50 or more than
open 2 positions long/short
1. Position A ( X Lots) SL 30 TP 30
2. Position B ( X Lots) SL 30 TP 45
Once the Position A Reaches its Profit target move position B SL to break even
Exits – Long And Short
The only time a position is closed is once its stop has been hit.

version 0.1

Routine
Monday to Friday arrive at the machine at 7.45.
Check on any previous positions from the previous Day
Calculate Appropriate lot sizes for the current Account Balance
Check the current MACD Signal on 240minute cable chart to see if its +/-
0.00050 difference from the 8pm signal
If The MACD difference is indicating to take a position and there are no
trades open then buy/sell 2 positions and set the correct SL and TP prices for
them both
Repeat again at 11.45 then at 15:45 and finally at 20:45
Trade Strategies, Setups, Entries and exits
Mechanical MACD system
===== PAGE 24/33 =====
The intention of this system is to capture 75 pips from a 45 pip movement in the
daily trend of currency pairs. The system allows for entry and exits to be easily
defined and to remove any negative affects that my emotions could have on the
outcome of the trade.
Settings
4 Hour chart
MACD (5,13,1)
Entries �  Long And Short
At 8am, 12pm, 4pm and 8pm check the chart to see if there is a positive or
negative difference of 0.00050 Between the 3 previous MACD signals.
If no trades are currently open. And If there is a positive/negative difference of 50
or more than open 2 positions long/short
 
1. Position A ( X Lots) SL 30 TP 30
===== PAGE 25/33 =====
2. Position B ( X Lots) SL 30 TP 45
Once the Position A Reaches its Profit target move position B SL to break even
Exits �  Long And Short
The only time a position is closed is once its stop has been hit.
Risk and Money management
I am prepared to risk a total of 5 % of my equity per trade. Or 2.5% per position.
I will use a online risk calculator to to calculate correct lot size for my account
equity, leverage and risk percentage.
http://www.goforex.net/money-management-calculator.htm
