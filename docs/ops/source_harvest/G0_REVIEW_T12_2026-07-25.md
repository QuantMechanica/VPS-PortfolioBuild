## QM5_20143 / STR-104

Approved as an explicitly weak, cost-sensitive falsification candidate:

- EURUSD.DWX and GBPUSD.DWX on M5; MACD zero state is the closed-bar
  EMA6-minus-EMA17 sign.
- A strict zero cross starts a campaign. A later close outside the causally
  aligned BB(10, plot shift 1, deviation 0.66) establishes extension; a later
  wick contact establishes pullback.
- A later strict close beyond the tracked pre-pullback extreme confirms the
  stop setup. Wick-only violations replace the reference extreme.
- Entry is one pip beyond the confirming candle, SL one pip beyond its
  opposite extreme, and TP exactly 1R. Invalid/behind-market broker geometry
  consumes the campaign rather than chasing or widening.
- One fill and one pending/position are permitted per campaign/magic. A GTC
  pending cancels on the opposite zero cross; news cancellation requires a
  fresh campaign breakout. A later zero cross does not close a filled
  position.
- Method 2 remains a labeled variant and discretionary Method 3 is excluded.

The hook keeps `Strategy_EntrySignal` false and owns the campaign/pending
automaton in `Strategy_ManageOpenPosition`. Its pool-registered raw iBands
handle is necessary because the framework reader fixes plot shift at zero.
An OnInit self-test proves that the value displayed on closed bar 1 is read
from source bar 2 and matches a manual ten-close population-deviation
calculation. Pending cancellation and broker placement attempts are paced by
forming-M5 latches. The custom news hook uses
`qm_news_temporal`/`qm_news_compliance` with `QM_NewsAllowsTrade2`, falling
back only to `qm_news_mode_legacy`.

its house projections:

- EURUSD.DWX, GBPUSD.DWX, USDJPY.DWX, and USDCHF.DWX; AUD pairs are excluded.
- Ichimoku 9/26/65 baseline, Tenkan strictly above/below Kijun, and the closed
  price more than one Wilder ATR20 beyond both causally displayed cloud spans.
  Settings 52 and 100 are the only labeled Span-B variants.
- State-based entry while flat; no fresh-cross requirement and no pyramiding.
- The initial stop is frozen at the signal bar's near cloud edge (house
  projection). Gap-invalid stop geometry skips the trade; there is no TP,
  Chikou condition, time exit, or trailing stop.
- A strict opposite Tenkan/Kijun cross closes at the next-bar market path.
- After a protective SL, that direction stays locked until its entry state is
  first false and later true again (house projection).

the cloud displayed on closed bar 1, it reads the native source at
`1 + displacement = 27`, then compares Span A with manual Tenkan/Kijun
midpoints and Span B with a manual 65-bar midpoint, all ending at bar 27.
Mismatch fails initialization. Manage owns protective-stop history
classification, the direction re-arm lock, and an opposite-cross close latch
that retains rejected intent but retries no more than once per D1 bar.
