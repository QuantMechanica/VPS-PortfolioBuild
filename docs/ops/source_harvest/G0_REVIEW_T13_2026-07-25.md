### Required volume-splittability audit

The reconciliation's stricter date-reject rule is enforced before either
pending is placed:

1. Compute the framework's exact risk-sized volume independently for the buy
   and sell stop using each pending entry and protected stop.
2. Normalize `0.50 * original_volume` with `QM_TM_NormalizeVolume`.
3. Require both halves to be legal, at least minimum volume, and to sum
   exactly to the normalized original within a volume-step tolerance.
4. After both orders are accepted, re-read each broker order's initial volume
   and repeat the proof. Any mismatch rolls back both orders and blocks the
   date.

At a representative 0.01 lot step/minimum, the truth-table check admitted
0.02, 0.04, and 0.10 lots and rejected 0.01, 0.03, and 0.05 lots. This is the
required payoff-preserving rejection behavior; it does not round an odd lot
into unequal tranches.
